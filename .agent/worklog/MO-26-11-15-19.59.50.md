exact fresh census later proved termination and replayed the structured
completed output locally, but the supervisor was only consuming the proof and
dropping the result.

is still supervised and the service lease is current, reverify the checkout
binding and emit only the declared files through the existing bounded reader
and acknowledged upload. Preserve proof-only recovery for all other cases.
