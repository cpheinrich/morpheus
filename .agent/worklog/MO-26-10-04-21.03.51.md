# MO-26-10-04-21.03.51 — Persist unsent overlay pins and the draft

## Why

Chris lost a half-written comment on 2026-10-04 when the overlay page reloaded after the agent
restarted the overlay server and re-navigated the Browser pane. His rule: comments persist for the
whole session, regardless of simulator or overlay restarts, until the batch is sent or the tab is
closed. Verified live after the fix: a pin plus partial text survived a reload with the "Restored 1
unsent pin" status, and Esc Esc then a reload left nothing behind.

## Independent review

A fresh Claude reviewer at small risk cleared the persistence change with two minor findings, one incidental note and no conditions. It re-ran the focused vitest file (18 pass), typecheck and lint, found the committed dist byte-identical to a fresh compile, and checked that restore tolerates malformed state, a failed Send keeps everything, the restored draft stays out of the pin until Enter, and the storage key cannot collide across projects or previews. F1: focusPin did not persist, so a reload could restore the wrong focused pin; fixed by persisting at the end of focusPin. F2: a saved blob whose pins all failed validation still reported Restored 0 unsent pins; fixed by forgetting the blob and returning false. F3 (incidental): pin numbers and createdAt were unvalidated for corrupted storage; fixed by requiring a positive integer n, defaulting createdAt, and deriving the next number from the highest restored one. All three landed in a335776dd6ca20662d49c1e14450f3fc75b23228 within the paths the findings name.
