# Codify simplification reviews and audits

The [runbook](../../docs/runbooks/simplification-audit.md) captures audit scoping, ranked
proposals, ownership and sequencing, validation, controller resumption, and measured closeout.
Its stages were replayed against a completed multi-lane refactor and a small CLI example.
Author and reviewer instructions link the workflow to the ordinary project lifecycle.
