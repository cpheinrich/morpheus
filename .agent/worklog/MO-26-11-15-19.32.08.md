# Keep Fleet workers through transient contact loss

Live attempts stopped before their recorded lease deadlines. The runner treated
every inspect or renewal exception as lost authority and immediately canceled the
active process. It then reconciled cleanup and recorded a generic terminal result.

The client now bounds the full request and distinguishes temporary connection and
service errors from a denial. An acknowledged renewal arms a monotonic watchdog
measured from before the request. The same live attempt can survive bounded
transient contact loss, while explicit refusal, cancellation and local lease
expiry still stop it. No new dependency or provider service operation is required.

## Independent review

Independent review cleared the exact lease repair after closing stalled-denial and late-renewal races. The final probe independently verified success before expiry, refusal at expiry, and bounded token and body waits.
