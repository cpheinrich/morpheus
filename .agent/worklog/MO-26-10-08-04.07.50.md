# Authorized follow-up satisfies the review floor

The 30-second normal/high-risk floor in `validateReviewRecord` fired on the initial turn whatever
followed, so an initial review honestly recorded `incomplete` for being under the floor could never
be resumed by the authorized same-reviewer turn the contract offers. Three workflow PRs hit this
(darwin-health/evo#412, cpheinrich/lakinacapital#509, cpheinrich/kairos#70); Chris authorized one
extra turn on each.

The record's top-level `outcome` is the final verdict, so there was nowhere to keep the initial
turn's own incomplete verdict once a follow-up completed the review. Added
`initialOutcome: "incomplete"` (a literal, not a general enum: nothing else needs it). Validation:

- An incomplete initial turn requires `humanAuthorization` on `followUps[0]`, mirroring the existing
  rule for incomplete follow-ups. It is exempt from the late-correction `scopeReason`, because it
  reopens no clearance.
- Under the floor at normal/high risk, the record is accepted only with `initialOutcome:
  "incomplete"` and an authorized follow-up of at least 30 s that clears. Same-reviewer, per-turn
  budgets and final clearance are the existing checks, unchanged.

The PR #354 worklog (MO-26-10-07-20.37.19) motivates the floor as "a pass this short is not a
review"; this change keeps that — the under-floor pass never counts, and the review that does count
must meet the floor itself.

Tests (`tests/local-review.test.ts`, new describe block): exact 30,000 ms follow-up accepted and
29,999 ms refused; under-floor initial recorded without `initialOutcome` refused (with and without a
scope reason); unauthorized follow-up, malformed `approvedAt`, different reviewer, no follow-up, and
a blocked authorized follow-up each refused; a 30,000 ms initial with nothing resumed still
accepted; an over-floor incomplete initial still needs authorization.

Validation: `pnpm exec vitest run tests/local-review.test.ts` (114 passed after review fixes), `pnpm typecheck`,
`pnpm compile`, `pnpm morpheus pm index`.

## Independent review

Independent high-risk review found the floor rescue sound except that an authorized finalization-only first follow-up could complete an incomplete (including under-floor) initial review, because the finalization predecessor check treated a missing previous turn as a clearance (F1, substantive), plus a fixture-only assertion (F2, minor). Both were fixed in e80ff84 and the same reviewer cleared them.

Same-reviewer follow-up cleared the F1 and F2 fixes. A finalization turn can no longer complete an incomplete initial review, by either the predecessor check or the floor-rescue predicate, and the preserved initial verdict is now asserted on the parsed record.

Limitation: in the follow-up turn the reviewer could not run Bash because its launch worktree had been removed; it read the changed files on disk and relied on the author's reported focused run (114/114) rather than re-running tests or `git diff` itself.
