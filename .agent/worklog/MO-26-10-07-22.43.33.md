# Review-turn audit and readiness policy

Audited the `morpheus-review` JSON records on current `origin/main` in Morpheus, Evo and Lakina, plus the changed review records on their open PR heads. Of 389 merged records, eight have more than three total turns: five Evo and three Lakina. Seven have an explicitly human-authorized additional code-review turn; Evo #394 used its fourth turn only for finalization. Morpheus has no merged record beyond three turns. No open PR head in the three repositories currently records an authorized extra turn.

The repeated causes were (1) focused tests, CI proof or simulator evidence exposing defects after clearance (Evo #308 and Lakina #429; Evo #409 also did this but was not in the merged sample), (2) new scope or integration after clearance (Evo #291, Lakina #467 and #468), and (3) substantive findings appearing across follow-ups in complex work (Evo #259, #308 and recovery phase 2). Evo #394's first reviewer lacked the full packet and later turns found additional minor issues; its fourth turn carried only the deferral record. One separate Evo record used human authorization within three total turns because a follow-up exceeded its time budget.

Policy response: finish and inspect relevant focused tests before the first reviewer turn; provide the whole packet and available UI evidence; keep an unvalidated PR draft. Aim for one review and one focused response, with the third as a ceiling. The author writes the ordinary review record. A finalization-only turn cannot fix missing timing or an incomplete verdict. The existing three-turn cap and narrow human exception remain for genuinely complex work. This changes the author workflow in Morpheus's own instructions and generated project instructions; it does not relax the checker.

Validation: `pnpm exec vitest run tests/agent-instructions.test.ts` passed (14 tests), `pnpm typecheck` passed, and `pnpm compile` passed. The focused test caught a scaffold-size regression during editing; the final generated instructions are below the existing size limit.
