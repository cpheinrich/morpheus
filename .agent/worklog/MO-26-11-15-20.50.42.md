# Carry Factory work through a bounded service interruption

Service request logs showed a 74-second gap during a Studio attempt. Its one-minute acknowledgement expired and stopped the worker while a local patch and focused test were in progress. The authority window now covers two minutes from an acknowledged renewal. The supervisor accounts for transport elapsed time and stops at the confirmed cutoff. Cancellation, attempt identity and owned-process proof still govern authority and release.

Apply the service revision first, then update idle supervisors. To roll back, drain and restore the supervisors before routing service traffic back.
