# HQ experience guide

HQ is an operating interface over dense company records. It uses the product's brand and design
system, but optimizes for rapid orientation, comparison and evidence rather than marketing.

## Reading hierarchy

Serve three depths: five-second orientation, thirty-second executive understanding and deliberate
study. The first screen states purpose, status/freshness, takeaway and action/decision. Long records
use an authored executive brief, stable local outline and bottom appendices for methods, exhaustive
tables, commands, provenance and history.

Choose one page type—operating hub, decision/research record, procedure, or data exploration—and
use its order. A complete directory mirror follows the current operating layer; it does not replace
it. Bound related navigation instead of listing every sibling.

## Brevity

Current direction is a verb-led outcome of at most ten words, with implementation detail in the
linked record. Keep an executive brief to 75 words or five one-sentence bullets. For a verbose
human-facing legacy page, target a 40–60% prose reduction while preserving the decision or action,
owner, deadline, thresholds, material evidence, caveats and compliance constraints. Shorten repetition and
setup before evidence. Move methods, provenance, history and exhaustive examples to appendices.

Do not compress legal/compliance documents or agent-centered records such as roadmap tickets,
worklogs, inboxes, agent logs and raw meeting/source records. If those records are rendered for
people, add a concise summary layer and leave the authoritative detail intact.

## Shared primitives

Prefer the project's `morpheus-kit` owners for shell, executive brief, in-page navigation,
breadcrumbs, related pages, data table, chart frame, metric card and status badge. Projects retain
authorization, route/catalogue choices, data transforms, copy and domain-specific explanatory
graphics.

A data table owns its caption/region, sort/filter/help controls, focusable overflow, announcements,
column priority and responsive mode. Default columns answer the page's question; exhaustive values
may use detail or download. A chart owns title/question, units, legend/tooltip tokens, accessible
summary, exact-data fallback and reduced motion. Use Recharts when conventional interactivity helps;
do not force domain diagrams into it.

## Migration

Inventory routes and resolve duplicate ownership first. Build the shell/content foundation, then
prove it with one dense table, one long procedure and one compact dashboard/record. Review the real
integrated builds at desktop, compact desktop/tablet and 390px mobile before a second consumer makes
the extraction real. Migrate active operational pages before archives and preserve stable URLs and
source meaning.

The full living standard is `docs/runbooks/hq-product-design.md` in Morpheus. When the installed
guide and the current Morpheus standard differ, update Morpheus and use the reviewed newer version;
do not fork local guidance silently.
