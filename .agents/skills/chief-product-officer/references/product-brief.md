# Product brief contract

Use this shape for a durable product mandate. Keep the body short enough to review; link evidence
rather than pasting the research corpus into the brief.

## Header

- **Outcome:** the user/business change, not the implementation.
- **Owner and period:** one accountable product owner and the decision horizon.
- **Status:** discovery, committed, building, validating, adopted, stopped.
- **Current call:** the one decision currently in force.

## Problem and evidence

- **Audience and situation:** who encounters the problem and when.
- **Observed behavior:** current completion, failure, delay or workaround.
- **Cost:** user harm, missed value, risk or operating burden.
- **Evidence:** direct research, product data, support evidence and important limits.
- **Why now:** what changed, deadline, compounding cost or strategic opportunity.

Separate observed facts, interpretations and assumptions. Name the weakest assumption whose failure
would change the decision.

## Outcome and boundaries

- **Target behavior:** what the audience can or does do when this works.
- **Success measures:** baseline, target, population, window and data owner.
- **Guardrails:** accessibility, safety, privacy, reliability, trust and business measures that must
  not regress.
- **Scope:** the smallest coherent journey included now.
- **Non-goals:** plausible adjacent work deliberately excluded.
- **Constraints:** policy, platform, dependency, brand, deadline and migration requirements.

## Options and decision

Compare at least the current state and the recommended approach. Add a genuinely different option
when one exists. For each, state user value, confidence, cost, reversibility and major risk. Record
the selected option, why it wins now, and what evidence would reverse it.

## Delivery and learning

- design and technical owners;
- canary or cheapest decisive test;
- acceptance evidence at delivery, usability, adoption and outcome levels;
- release/migration sequence and rollback or safe stop;
- decisions requiring a person, with recommendation and deadline;
- next checkpoint and what will be examined.

## Closeout

Record the shipped or stopped state, observed result versus baseline, unintended effects, confidence,
remaining risk and follow-up owner. Link the final product, design, technical and research records.
