# Diagram layout acceptance

- The shared guide defaults flowcharts to top-to-bottom, permits compact horizontal layouts
  when helpful, and asks agents to inspect the render at reading width before delivery.
- A wide baseline and revised reporting flow demonstrate the change at desktop width. A narrow
  pipeline, a decision with a retry, and a short sequence remain readable in representative
  chat-width previews. Inspect labels, arrows, grouping, overlap, and contrast.
- Existing distribution tests verify that the Claude mirror and CLI use the canonical text.
  The planning trigger and fenced Mermaid documentation convention remain intact.
