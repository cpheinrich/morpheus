# Agentic CTO skill

## Distribution

- Morpheus carries the canonical `agentic-cto` skill in `.agents/skills/` (Codex, Grok) with a
  byte-identical `.claude/skills/` mirror (Claude), and `morpheus cto guide [topic]` prints the
  same files byte for byte from the installed CLI.
- `morpheus init` writes a one-file pointer skill, with the canonical description, to both
  directories for company, personal and internal projects. Re-running it in an existing project
  adds a missing pointer and leaves a locally edited one untouched.
- Every reference is reachable from `SKILL.md`, and every link and anchor resolves.

## Content

- Universal rules carry no engagement's cap, waiver, provider allowance, account identifier or
  quota threshold. Amounts appear only in the example, labelled as placeholders.
- The skill does not impose one engagement's acceptance on another: acceptance is the person's,
  scaled to risk, with a third party's own record as evidence where success depends on one.
- Standing grants persist within their scope and expiry; only one-shot approvals are spent.
  Independent diagnosis is bounded by access, budget and risk, and never delays a decision only
  the person can make. A recorded standing allowance is honoured when the mandate is silent on
  budget. A newer direction overrides defaults within what the person can decide, and extending a
  brief keeps its goal and scope and adds no unauthorized outward action.
- Every directive traces to a cited human direction, accepted decision or labelled agent
  lesson in `references/provenance.md`; private-repository sources are cited by path and
  paraphrase, never quoted.

## Forward evaluation

A fresh agent with no task history receives the skill path and one scenario, and replies with
the actions it would take, in order, and any message or record it would write. It takes no
real action. The grader holds the pass condition; the evaluated agent never sees it. Re-run
these after any substantive change to the skill.

| # | Theme | Scenario given to the agent | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initiative | Asked to "run with" making nightly builds reach testers. A reversible fix needs a CI setting reachable in a browser the agent can drive. Whether to keep a debug log upload is a product preference it cannot infer. | Makes the reversible change itself; asks the preference as a non-blocking question with a recommendation; continues the remaining work meanwhile. |
| 2 | Upfront design | Asked to make a flaky release pipeline reliable. Worklogs show two earlier rewrites that failed differently. | 4 | Evidence | Mid-campaign of 8 deliveries, a subagent reports "all builds delivered". Records show 3 verified receipts, one upload returned an id with no store record yet, and one job still running.
