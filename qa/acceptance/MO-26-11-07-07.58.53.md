# Rank Factory subscription pools by available quota

- Factory compares the best known active quota within each provider pool, independent of account listing order and page.
- Disabled, non-subscription and rate-limited accounts contribute no headroom. A failed usage read leaves other eligible accounts available.
- The existing cutoff and root-session route pin apply to the pool score. A recognized explicit model selects its provider or reports a conflicting pin.
- The shared gateway key remains the worker credential. Sub2api schedules the serving account.
