## Context

Morpheus had nowhere to keep context produced by collaboration between people — meetings, chat,
who the collaborators even are. Everything captured so far was either agent↔human (`hq/team/`
inboxes) or work state (`hq/product/`). This meeting settled where that material lives and what
shape it takes.

## Agenda

- Where `meeting-notes/` should sit
- Whether the inbox moves
- What the heartbeat should do about meeting context

## Discussion

The folder question turned out to be the least consequential part. `hq/` is organised by business
function — product, brand, finance — and a meeting is not a function: one meeting covers three. The
tell that a second category already existed is that inboxes sat at the top of `hq/` rather than
under a function, because they record communication rather than hold work.

The load-bearing question was raw versus distilled. A transcript is high volume and low signal, and
storing transcripts would make an agent's context worse — it would read more and know less. Settled
that notes are **always summaries**, written by whoever is in the meeting, into a canonical format
with a redaction pass.

On the heartbeat: it cannot reach Granola or iMessage from a CI runner, so it reports the *gap* —
how stale the notes are, and which produced no roadmap items — and an interactive session does the
ingestion.

## Decisions

- **`hq/team/` holds collaborative context**, with inboxes at its root rather than in a
  subdirectory. A person is the primary thing in the folder; a medium should not sit above one.
- **Meeting notes are summaries, never transcripts.** The redaction pass is a schema field rather
  than a convention, so CI refuses a note that has not been through it.
- **A note's id comes from when the meeting started**, not when the note was written — otherwise
  notes sort by when somebody got round to writing them.
