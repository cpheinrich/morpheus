## Context

Launching a company crosses registrar, DNS, source control, Google Cloud,
Firebase, and Vercel boundaries. The deterministic commands exist, but a new
agent needs the ordering, authorization gates, and acceptance criteria at the
moment a greenfield request arrives.

## Approach

Add a narrowly triggered `launch-company` skill that inventories before it
creates, delegates deterministic work to Morpheus commands, protects secrets,
and finishes only after both the public origin and role-gated `/hq` work from a
user's perspective. Document the skill's ownership in the architecture.
