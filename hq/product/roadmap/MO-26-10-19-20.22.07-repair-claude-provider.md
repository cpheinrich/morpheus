## Context

The first real Evo hosted capability probe (39363087252) failed before quota or
model invocation because the shared App exchange rejected its response. The
adapter restricts token characters beyond the official action contract. Separately,
quota normalization discards model-specific windows, so it cannot establish the
explicit Opus capacity Chris requested when Fable is saturated. Actual response
shape and Opus allowance remain unproven until a reviewed consumer probe succeeds.

## Approach

Honor opaque single-line App credentials, masking safely and rejecting injection
before output. Preserve OIDC/default-branch/provider checks, scoped capability
proof and revocation. Join shared windows with the explicitly configured Claude
model-family window; reject unknown/null/missing/stale capacity and mismatched or
incomplete model telemetry files. Keep consumer policy/config migration separate.

## Acceptance

- Opaque dotted/punctuated credentials and empty-primary/valid-fallback responses
  preserve exact token bytes in protected output, with safe masking only.
- Invalid credentials cannot alter output or leak values in logs/errors.
- Opus admission considers shared and Opus limits, excludes unrelated model limits,
  and rejects unavailable or incomplete capacity evidence.
- Reviewed consumer execution still proves actual App authority and relevant quota
  before activation; no model calls, PAT fallback or live delivery credit here.
