## Context

Apple can report VALID processing before its internal beta build becomes assignable. Promoting UNKNOWN readiness caused a real404 and stalled an accepted upload.

## Approach

Gate internal group promotion on explicit beta readiness, preserving bounded diagnosis and exact receipt validation. Reproduce UNKNOWN → ready → membership readback with one upload and no replacement dispatch.

## Acceptance

- Non-ready beta states never assign.
- Later readiness promotes the same build; delivery requires group readback.
- Prolonged unreadiness retains accepted bytes and invokes bounded diagnosis.
