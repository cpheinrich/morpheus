## Context

#376 replaced `gray-matter` with `@11ty/gray-matter`. The old js-yaml 3 turned an unquoted
`date: 2026-08-18T13:15:00.000Z` into a `Date`, which `isoDate` sliced to its day; the new parser
returns the string, `z.iso.date()` refuses a datetime, and `inbox validate` fails on every Darwin
inbox written with a timestamp. Darwin's trunk CI has failed since 2026-10-24 01:47Z
(cpheinrich/morpheus#468).

## Approach

Make `isoDate` accept an ISO datetime string and slice it to its date, exactly what the legacy
Date path did, with a test over both spellings; refresh the committed `dist/`.
