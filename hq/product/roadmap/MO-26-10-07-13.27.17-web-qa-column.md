## Context

Chris's first real web QA session (Lakina, 2026-10-07) found four problems: the comments sat in a
small dark floating box instead of a full-height column like the iOS overlay; Enter saved silently,
so it looked broken; the box was dark on a light site; and the batch he sent was never picked up,
because the agent had not armed the inbox watch the guide prescribes and nothing prompted it to.

## Approach

- A top-level page load gets a shell: the site in a same-origin frame, a full-height comment column
  on the right (list, composer, Send), the iOS layout. Framed requests are the injected site.
- The column takes the site's computed background, ink and font and follows theme changes.
- Enter saves with visible feedback; Esc Esc deletes; ⌘Enter sends from the column or the page.
- `start` tells the agent to arm the inbox watch; the guide says to do it before handing over.

Acceptance: on Lakina's HQ through the overlay, pin, Enter, theme toggle and ⌘Enter send all work,
the frame shows the site without the column, and navigation and reload keep the framed page.
