## Context

The production dependency graph resolves `brace-expansion@5.0.9` through the public
`minimatch` dependency. It carries two high-severity recursion/stack-exhaustion advisories and one
moderate quadratic-expansion advisory. `minimatch@10.2.6` already permits the patched 5.0.12
release, but the lockfile retained the vulnerable patch.

## Approach

Pin the transitive package to the smallest release that clears all three advisories, regenerate
the lockfile, and verify the production audit plus Morpheus's complete Node validation.

## Result

The workspace now resolves `brace-expansion@5.0.12` on the production path. The production audit
is clean and the override stays within `minimatch@10.2.6`'s declared semver range.
