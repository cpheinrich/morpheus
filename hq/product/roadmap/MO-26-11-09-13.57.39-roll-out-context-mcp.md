## Context

Existing Morpheus checkouts can have the current CLI while lacking project-local
Context MCP registrations. Collaborators who have enabled auto-update need the
search tools in existing projects, worktrees and new clones.

## Approach

Reconcile device-local MCP registrations through the opted-in auto-update path.
Preserve user-owned settings and keep repositories clean. Verify a fresh client
can obtain cited results from the right project on both qualification machines.
