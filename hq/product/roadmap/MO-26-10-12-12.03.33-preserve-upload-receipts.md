## Context

An accepted IPA can be followed by processing or distribution failure, making the combined step unsuitable as an upload cursor (#299).

## Approach

Persist allowlisted per-attempt acceptance evidence before post-upload work, without release credentials. Retain prior attempts, refuse ambiguous API results, and keep missing evidence uncertain.

## Acceptance

- Pre-upload failure produces no acceptance.
- Confirmed acceptance survives processing and group-assignment failures.
- Failed retries cannot overwrite earlier receipts.
- Artifacts contain exact source/run/build identity and no credentials; distribution starts only after persistence.
