## Context

The authorized CI host is below its storage reserve. Audit actual use and protected
candidates, reclaim obsolete generated data through its owner, and qualify one macOS CI VM
with 8 vCPU / 16384 MiB and two native Xcode workers. The larger host retains its two
6-vCPU / 16384-MiB VMs and two native workers per VM.

## Approach

Factory host cleanup remains in Morpheus; CI image/cache lifetimes remain in
`cpheinrich/agent-host-setup`. Extend only measured closed-app cache roots, share the
dispatcher's cache-path identity with offline pruning, and preserve active caches,
configured images, source, sessions, credentials, controller receipts and gateway services.

Source preparation stops for independent supervisor review before merge or installation.
Protected merge/CI, host-local cutover, actual free-space recovery and a real test-only CI
completion remain operational acceptance. No TestFlight upload or Apple action is authorized
by this qualification. Private host identifiers, candidate paths and rollout commands stay
in the ignored handoff. No new paid service, provider override or scheduler.

Evo EV-26-11-06-14.05.11 supplies the Mini-only manual CI runner label and corrects serial-only
guidance. Qualification uses the ordinary all-unit scope and proves configured cap2/native
YES/max2, reporting Xcode's observed worker count without forcing clones or a full suite.
Host-setup's existing installer attempts offline cache retirement without blocking startup
when active/uncertain ownership defers it; images remain an explicit post-qualification action.
