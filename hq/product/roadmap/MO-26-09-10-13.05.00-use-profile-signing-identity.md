## Context

The Mac mini proves that the distribution leaf is current, chains through WWDR
G3 to Apple Root CA, and passes Apple's code-signing trust policy. The PKCS#12
import also reports an identity, yet `security find-identity` returns zero on
the headless account. That enumeration is the only failed assertion.

## Approach

Validate the actual release contract directly: require the profile-authorized
leaf certificate in the isolated keychain, require a private signing key, and
verify the leaf under the code-signing trust policy. Pin Xcode export to the
profile certificate's SHA-1 fingerprint. The export and subsequent signature
verification remain the end-to-end proof that the imported key can sign.
