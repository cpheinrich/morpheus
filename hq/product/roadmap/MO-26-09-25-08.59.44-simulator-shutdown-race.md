## Context

Cross-project review found simctl shutdown can fail if XCTest already completed shutdown after inventory. The action then skipped deletion, leaving its owned device behind. Follow-up to #266 and #267/#268.

## Approach

Attempt exact-owned deletion independently of shutdown success in both default and testing device sets. Keep deletion/inventory failures visible and continue across other owned devices.
