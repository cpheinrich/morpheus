## Context

Morpheus Security now creates validated dependency PRs, but Morpheus's target policy requires
`agent-review / delivery` to pass even though CI intentionally reports it as skipped for the
exact bot lane. The bot's own success-only gate therefore waits forever after other checks pass.

## Approach

Remove the skipped check from the bot's local `requiredChecks` while retaining GitHub branch
protection's required check. Document and test that distinction, then verify a later bot
reconciliation can merge a green candidate without weakening ordinary PR review.
