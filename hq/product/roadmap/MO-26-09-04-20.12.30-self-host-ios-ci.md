## Context

GitHub-hosted macOS runners for Evo and Kairos consumed roughly $200 in one week. The dedicated
Mac mini already carries the required Xcode and simulator toolchain, so routine iOS CI and
TestFlight builds should use that capacity while preserving GitHub-hosted macOS as an explicit
recovery path.

## Approach

Add a stable self-hosted runner contract to the reusable iOS workflows, support the Mac mini's
canonical Xcode application path, and document the trust boundary. Register repo-scoped runner
services under an isolated non-admin macOS account, with a host-wide lock so Evo and Kairos cannot
run simulator workloads concurrently. Migrate callers only after canary runs pass, and retain a
manual GitHub-hosted backstop for runner outages.
