## Context

Kairos's native tests require Firebase Auth and Firestore emulators plus a repository-owned fixture
script. The reusable nightly release called `ios-ci` without forwarding that established test
contract, so a thin Kairos caller would either test a different surface before release or duplicate
the orchestration Morpheus is meant to own.

## Approach

Expose the existing `ios-ci` Firebase and parallel-test inputs on `nightly-ios-build`, forward them
unchanged, and expose an optional protected Google service plist only to the caller-owned upload
step. Pin both seams with parsed-workflow tests and document the caller/reusable ownership boundary.
