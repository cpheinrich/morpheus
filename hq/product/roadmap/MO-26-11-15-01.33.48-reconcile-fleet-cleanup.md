## Context

A completed Fleet worker can retain an unverified cleanup result after its descendants exit. A held workspace then blocks subsequent jobs until an operator runs an exact process census.

## Approach

Use the existing non-signaling census in supervisor recovery for the exact retained task, attempt and admission. Preserve the original record and continue holding when proof is missing or a process survives.

## Acceptance

- A verified stopped worker releases its Fleet slot on recovery.
- Foreign, incomplete and still-live admissions cannot obtain a cleanup proof.
- Recovery never signals processes, changes the retained guardian record or replays a launch.
