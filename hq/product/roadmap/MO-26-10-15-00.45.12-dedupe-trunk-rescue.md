## Context

Evo's Xcode-open trunk generated seven rescue draft PRs in 36 hours, many
with byte-identical project or scheme diffs. The rescue preserved each edit
correctly, but repeated the same PR and human escalation.

## Approach

Compare exact Git diffs, including old/new blob ids, paths and modes, with
locally visible pushed `wip/trunk-*` branches before opening a new rescue.
Keep the existing proof and reset sequence. A match resets trunk and reports
the earlier branch plus the generator-loop cause; a missing/failed comparison
continues the normal rescue. Do not suppress a new rescue against a local-only
branch whose push failed.
