## Context

Evo shipped consumer accounts with three emulator-backed test suites and a standalone CI workflow
(darwin-health/evo#58, #62). The workflow carries pins that were each paid for in a failed run —
JDK 21 because ubuntu-latest's JRE is too old for firebase-tools 15 and the failure only appears in
CI, the emulator-jar cache keyed on `firebase.json`, the Playwright browser cache keyed on the
lockfile, `--with-deps`, trace upload on failure — and the whole thing runs without secrets, so it
passes on fork pull requests. Its own header says it is worth lifting upstream once a second
project needs Firebase testing; cpheinrich/morpheus#135 is the umbrella.

Evo also runs two Firebase projects with staging as the default, and nothing in the manifest could
record the staging pair — so tooling would have to infer it from a deployment.

## Approach

Lift the workflow as `.github/workflows/firebase-tests.yml` with `on: workflow_call` and inputs for
the app directory, the two root script names, the token build, and the firebase-tools / JDK pins.
Deliberately separate from `web-ci.yml`: most projects have no Firebase and would pay for a JRE, a
100 MB jar and an emulator boot to run nothing.

Add `stagingDomain` and the `accounts.gcpProjectStaging` / `accounts.firebaseStaging` convention to
the manifest schema, held to the same origin shape as `publicDomain`, and record the two-project
policy in `architecture.md` §13.2. The `web add-consumer-auth` scaffold (the follow-up on #135)
reads these rather than inferring origins.
