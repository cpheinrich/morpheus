## Context

Issues #217 and #232 reproduce the same failure: a cancelled self-hosted run leaves
Build.xcresult or Tests.xcresult, and xcodebuild refuses the next run before any test.
Old logs and screenshots can also survive as misleading new-run artifacts.

## Approach

Remove only Results, Logs and Screenshots before recreating per-run directories. Preserve
SourcePackages and DerivedData. Execute the real workflow preparation script against a fresh
runner and repeated cancelled-run fixtures, pinning cleanup, cache survival and exported paths.
