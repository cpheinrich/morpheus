## Context

MO-043 made `check pr` block a PR that changes only records and board files from sitting on a
branch that stakes a roadmap id. But a gate only covers what passes through it, and all three
historical instances merged green because the rule did not exist yet.

Reconcile is the last line and trusted the branch name completely: a merged PR whose head branch
carries an item's prefix marked that item shipped, whatever the PR contained. That is the
mechanism that wrote `status: shipped, prs: [31]` onto MO-010.

Raised in the inbox; Chris had no strong preference and left the call here.

## Approach

Apply the same predicate from the other side, and — the part that makes it worth doing — apply
*the same predicate*, not a second copy. My original objection to this change was that two places
deciding one thing will eventually disagree. Moving `hasNoSubstantiveChange` to `src/paths.ts`
answers that: one definition, two callers, no duplication. `check/pr.ts` already imports from
`pm/`, so it could not live in either.

A new `no-work` outcome reports without writing, consistent with how `reopened` already behaves —
reconcile declines to guess rather than guessing conservatively.

`files === null` means the list could not be read and shipping proceeds. Refusing on an unread
list would stall every reconcile the day `gh` renames a field, and this guard needs positive
evidence rather than the absence of it.
