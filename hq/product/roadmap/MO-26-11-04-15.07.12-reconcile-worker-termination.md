## Context

An unattended gateway-worker admission is terminal only when the guardian proved its owned
processes gone at exit (`process.json` `cleanupVerified: true`). The guardian decides that once,
in a few seconds after the worker exits. A census error, an identity ambiguity, or a detached tool
that outlives the window leaves `cleanupVerified: false` for good: `--status` reports termination
unverified forever, `--resume` is refused, and the guardian's workspace admission for the next
invocation refuses too. This was observed on a real exited admission whose recorded owned
processes and guardian were all gone minutes later. There is no supported way to re-prove it.

## Approach

An explicit `gateway-worker --reconcile ADMISSION_ID`:
- runs a fresh, read-only census through the existing owned-process tracker (no signals);
- writes one exclusive-create `reconciliation.json` only when everything checks out, bound by
  SHA-256 to that admission's `process.json` and `admission.json` bytes.

The census is seeded with the recorded identities. It covers the run marker, those identities and
their descendants, and the recorded guardian identity.

One Python validator checks the proof. The guardian's admission gate and `workerResult` both use
it, so `--status` and `--resume` accept a valid proof. `--status` stays observational.
`process.json` is never modified.

## Acceptance

- An owned process still alive under its recorded identity, an unrecorded process carrying the
  marker, or the recorded guardian still alive: the reconcile is refused and no process is
  signalled.
- After the owned processes exit, the proof is written; status reports termination verified;
  `process.json` bytes and timing are unchanged.
- A PID reused by an unrelated process is not treated as owned and is never signalled.
- Fail closed, with no proof written, for any of these:
  - an unreadable census;
  - a malformed or missing identity or ownership record;
  - the wrong run;
  - a record still running, rejected or never launched;
  - a record whose cleanup was already verified.
- A proof whose bound snapshot changed is rejected by status and by the guardian.
- Repeating the reconcile is idempotent.
- The public CLI `--reconcile`, then `--resume` through the Python guardian with a local fake
  executor, resumes the retained session on the same binding without a duplicate admission. The
  same flow is refused before the change.
