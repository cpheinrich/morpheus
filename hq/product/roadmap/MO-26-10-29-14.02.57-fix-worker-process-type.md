## Context

The CD V2 worker LaunchAgent template sets `ProcessType` `Adaptive`. With no XPC client launchd
treats that as background work (priority 4, CPU and I/O throttled). On a consumer Mac mini the
throttled `codex app-server` missed its consumer shim's 10-second initialize deadline on almost
every scheduled wake, so the quota reader failed and repair work stayed in a telemetry budget wait
while the account was healthy (#558). A repair worker is latency-sensitive work an operator waits
on, not opportunistic background work.

## Approach

Render the worker with `ProcessType` `Interactive`, document why, and have the host integration
test refuse any other value. The tick keeps `Background`: it is short, deterministic and spawns no
provider handshake. The optional stderr tail in #558 is already covered by the sanitized
fixed-vocabulary reader diagnostic merged in #554; a raw tail would contradict the 2026-10-19
fixed-diagnostics decision, so none is added. Consumers re-render under their own ops review.
