## Context

Issue #478 reports an exact roadmap ID returning an unrelated numeric-heavy inbox first. MiniSearch splits the timestamp into separately scored tokens, and the ordinary roadmap-history source weight worsens the mismatch.

## Approach

Keep the existing MiniSearch index and ordinary scoring, but rank exact record identities first under the default policy. Match a complete title/path/basename or a complete roadmap ID at the filename boundary; body mentions and partial numeric matches do not qualify. Preserve explicit project ranking callbacks and payload compatibility.

## Acceptance

- The reported LK-26-08-28-13.52.42 query returns its roadmap record before numeric-heavy inboxes and body mentions, including after serialization and with a result limit.
- Exact title/path lookups prefer their record; partial identifiers are not exact matches.
- Ordinary topic searches and explicit project ranking callbacks retain their existing behavior.
