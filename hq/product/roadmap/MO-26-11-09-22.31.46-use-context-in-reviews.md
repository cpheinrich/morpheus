## Context

Reviewers and subagents often bypassed project record search. The engine experiment was no longer useful after the recall and timing comparison.

## Approach

Make MCP and CLI use Tantivy, provision the runtime on update, and carry the MCP-first cue through native child hooks, review packets and Factory handoffs. Keep historical and current profiling.
