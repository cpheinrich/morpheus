## Context

A tranche of workflow fixes is about to be measured with `morpheus profile`, and two gaps make
that measurement wrong. Codex compresses older rollouts in place to `.jsonl.zst` and moves some to
`~/.codex/archived_sessions/`; the profiler read only plain `.jsonl` under `~/.codex/sessions/`, so
re-extracting 2026-10-09 to 10-21 found zero Evo Codex main sessions where an earlier run found 14.
Separately, the trivial-diff review-floor waiver (#365) is applied by the validator without
printing anything, so nobody can tell how often it fires. Unparseable transcript lines are also
reported as a bare count with no file.

## Approach

Read Codex rollouts from both `sessions/` and `archived_sessions/`, plain or zstd-compressed,
deduplicated by session id, and report how many sessions came from each source and provider.
Have `check pr` and `review validate` print the floor waiver with its measured line counts, and
name the files (and lines) behind every skipped transcript issue.

Acceptance: overlapping archived and live fixtures count once; the report shows per-source counts;
the waiver line appears at exactly 20 non-test / 40 test lines and not one line over.
