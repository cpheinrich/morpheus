## Context

The GitHub Manager waits eight hours for a ready PR, then may queue auto-merge. After 48 hours it
may review a draft, mark it ready, and merge it. Chris found that WIP PRs often need more input and
must remain under their authors' control.

## Approach

Hold drafts, `wip/` branches, and titles beginning `[WIP]` or `WIP:` out of the merge and session
routes. Cancel already queued auto-merge on them. Recheck the live PR before applying any session
decision or deterministic merge route. Keep rescued trunk drafts' human escalation. Change the
ready PR quiet period to 24 hours and cover the boundary and WIP transitions in focused tests.
