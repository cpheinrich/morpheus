## Context

Issue #765 shows that a term present in both ordinary and branded ASO inputs loses its branded flag because ordinary terms are deduplicated first.

## Approach

Merge branded membership into the first normalized keyword row. Preserve spelling, order, one lookup per distinct term, and the existing 3–5 distinct-term bounds. Synthetic fetch regressions cover exact overlap, case/whitespace differences, branded-only duplicates, and 2/3/5/6-term boundaries. See `.agent/worklog/2026-11-23-preserve-branded-terms.md`.
