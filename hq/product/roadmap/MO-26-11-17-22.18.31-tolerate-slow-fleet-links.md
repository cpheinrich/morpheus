## Context

An enrolled worker lost its lease on a degraded network link. Authenticated
worker requests on the same host completed in 8.5–8.6 seconds when allowed
to finish, beyond the eight-second deadline. A later claim on this link
was also recovered as never launched.

## Approach

Give the worker control calls 20 seconds end to end. Preserve the supervisor's
independent cutoff based on its last acknowledged grant, along with existing
controller and artifact deadlines. Build on the [two-minute lease](MO-26-11-15-20.50.42-factory-lease-duration.md).

## Acceptance

- Worker control calls have time to complete on the measured slow link.
- Controller and artifact deadlines remain bounded as before.
- An expired or canceled attempt still cannot renew or continue.
- Verify the updated supervisor using an authenticated read followed by normal
  job admission and a running worker on the affected slot.
