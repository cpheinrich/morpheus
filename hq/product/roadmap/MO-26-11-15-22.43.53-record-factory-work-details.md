## Context

Factory Work can display viewer-safe descriptions, but routine submissions have been omitting them. Historical queue and Recent rows need the same context.

## Approach

Have the submitting controller provide a short display title and purpose summary. Preserve the execution identity when annotating historical records. See [the original Work projection](MO-26-11-15-01.45.04-describe-factory-work.md).

## Acceptance

- New interactive Fleet submissions supply a viewer-safe title and summary while local work and existing Fleet supervision retain their contracts.
- Retrying the same brief and execution binding after a display edit returns the same task without overwriting the stored copy.
- Backfilled Darwin and Evo titles and summaries appear through the existing Infra Work projection.
