## Context

Chris authorized the Evo delivery incident lead to fix automatic recovery and all audited failure modes. A protected M430/E686 runtime is now actually adopted, but a genuine ordinary Mini worker still returns `configured reader exited (code=2)` during initialization. The consumer helper emits fixed sanitized startup phase/kind diagnostics, while nativeQuota drains and discards its stderr. Controlled direct and supervised initialize-only probes succeed; the actual scheduled cause remains unresolved. Preserving the helper's already sanitized diagnostic removes this verified observability gap without changing admission authority.

## Acceptance

- Retain only the exact recognized fixed helper diagnostic grammar: enumerated startup phase/kind and bounded optional errno/exit code. Arbitrary provider stderr, secret sentinels, trailing text, malformed/oversized lines and unsupported phases/numbers never enter returned errors, public logs or native state.
- Preserve a bounded recognized diagnostic in the worker's private error stream, correlated with the reader and elapsed phase, including when stderr arrives after the failure control status. Include it in the normal quota error when already available; asynchronous ordering must not hide the diagnostic entirely.
- Keep fail-fast behavior, quota/model stop timing, unknown-usage refusal, private group cleanup, RPC/reader deadlines, custody and configuration/version/permission/connector guards unchanged. No account/login/model/provider request is needed for validation.
- Focused tests assert exact safe output/redaction and both cross-stream orderings, duplicate and chunk/line bounds, numeric/enum boundaries, timeout/cleanup behavior and zero skips. Selected guard mutants must fail assertions and be restored. Typecheck and compile required; full suite belongs to CI.
- Follow the normal independent HIGH review, meaningful CI and protected non-admin merge. Source completion is not consumer adoption, runtime recovery, TestFlight delivery or proof-before-blocking consumer enforcement. Record the required subsequent Evo binding/runtime work separately.

## Approach

Extend the existing quota-reader diagnostic boundary with a small domain-specific bounded parser and one fixed private diagnostic emission. Prefer local built-in string/stream handling to a generic logging package; do not redesign the supervisor or delay an admission failure merely to wait for stderr. No native stderr dumps. The incident lead retains the actual failure/probe proofs privately; public records contain the mechanism and verification only.
