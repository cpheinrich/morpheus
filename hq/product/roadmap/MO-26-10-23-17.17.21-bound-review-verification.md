## Context

Chris requested a modest relaxation after repeated PRs stalled on one late, narrow correction. The three ordinary turns remain the default; this item proposes one additional constrained same-reviewer verification turn. Historical outcomes and finalization-only bookkeeping retain their existing meaning.

## Approach

Add an opt-in fourth turn only for a small fix to a prior finding or a late test-only correction. Require the original reviewer, a declared scope, measured time and a cumulative budget; mechanically check changed paths and line counts. Preserve finalization and explicit human exceptions. Cover accepted and rejected boundaries in focused tests, update the reviewer contract and architecture, and leave a draft PR for approval of the exact numeric limits before activation.
