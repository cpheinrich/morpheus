## Context

The reusable HQ guard treats presence in Next's prerender manifest as proof that repository content
is deploy-time-only. ISR routes are also present there, but may execute their loaders after the
revalidation interval expires. Evo's independent review reproduced that gap with
`initialRevalidateSeconds: 60` while migrating its HQ surface.

## Approach

Require `initialRevalidateSeconds === false` for every declared repository-backed static route,
including generated URLs. Add an explicit ISR regression fixture, update the reusable runbook and
scaffold, and prove the final compiled guard before the remaining project migrations consume it.
