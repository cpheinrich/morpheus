## Context

A shallow CD source checkout can assign ordinals below already-admitted sources and block a valid delivery.

## Approach

Refuse shallow source history before deriving ordinals or reading PR evidence. Provide the supported unshallow command and verify that it restores the same source ordinals as a full checkout.
