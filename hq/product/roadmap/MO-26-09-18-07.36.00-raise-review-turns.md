## Context

The independent-review contract allowed two turns: the initial review and one same-reviewer
follow-up for substantive findings. A follow-up that returned `blocked` on a concern the author
could actually resolve left no move short of a human, because the second turn was also the last.
Chris asked on 2026-09-18 to raise the cap to three turns while keeping the purpose of the cap: an
author and a reviewer trading fixes and findings indefinitely is unproductive and burns credits,
so only substantive unresolved concerns after the last turn leave a PR open for human judgment.

## Approach

Record follow-up turns as an ordered `followUps` array of at most two entries in the
`morpheus-review` worklog fence. Every turn but the last must have returned `blocked`, the last
must be `cleared` on `covered`, each stays within the follow-up ceiling, and turn commits form an
ancestor chain. The legacy single `followUp` object still validates as one turn so existing
records and documentation-integration sources keep working. Update the runbook, the reviewer
prompt, `AGENTS.md` and the scaffold template together with the committed CLI.

## Acceptance

- A substantive review whose first follow-up returned `blocked` can be cleared by a second
  follow-up from the same reviewer, and `check pr` passes.
- A second follow-up after a `cleared` or `incomplete` first follow-up is refused, as is any
  third follow-up.
- Each follow-up is held to half the initial ceiling at its boundary.
- Existing records with a single `followUp` validate unchanged.
