## Context

Chris is using the Lakina, Darwin and Evo HQ portals more heavily and repeatedly finds the same
class of problems: dense pages obscure their takeaway, document structure is hard to skim, and
one-off tables, charts and controls drift from the design system. Fixing individual pages does not
stop those failures from recurring. The shared Morpheus guidance and product/design roles need to
make clear, compact and branded HQ experiences the default while preserving useful depth.

## Approach

Audit a representative cross-project sample in the rendered browser and the source repositories;
review earlier formatting work and current shared rendering responsibilities; turn the evidence
into a prioritized HQ design/content standard and migration plan; then encode the reusable product
leadership judgment in first-draft Chief Product Officer and VP Design skills.

## Mandate — 2026-10-29, from Chris's request to “do your own audit and come up with a list of improvements”

**Outcome:** replace repeated one-off HQ formatting fixes with a reusable design, information-
architecture and component strategy for Morpheus projects, plus roles that can own future product
and design outcomes.

**Acceptance:**

- a browser-based audit covers representative Lakina, Darwin and Evo HQ home, document, table and
  visualization surfaces, including responsive behavior where material;
- findings distinguish shared-system causes from project-specific content and rank improvements by
  user impact and migration cost;
- an HQ guideline defines progressive disclosure, executive summaries, in-page navigation,
  appendices, typography/layout, tables, charts, responsive behavior, accessibility and reusable
  component expectations;
- a staged migration plan identifies ownership, inventories, pilots, validation and a low-disruption
  rollout path rather than silently rewriting every page at once;
- first-draft Chief Product Officer and VP Design skills encode the learned product/design operating
  model and are registered and validated like other reusable project skills;
- implementation is reviewed, passes focused validation and CI, and merges through the normal
  protected-branch workflow.

**Authority:** inspect the three local repositories and authenticated HQ pages; create and merge
Morpheus records, guidance, reusable skill definitions and their tests. This does not authorize
rewriting Lakina, Darwin or Evo pages in this item, changing live data, or deploying those projects.

**Budget:** no new paid services or metered API spend. Use the existing Codex subscription and
ordinary local/browser tooling. Factory routing remains disabled because Chris did not request it.

**Delegation:** ordinary subagents may perform bounded read-only audit/research work. The controller
owns synthesis, repository changes, review, CI and merge.

**Reporting and stop:** report material findings, decisions and completion; stop at the reviewed,
merged Morpheus standard and role drafts, with cross-project migrations filed as follow-up work.
