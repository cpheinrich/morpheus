## Context

Morpheus went public to remove friction, not on principle. Chris wants minimal public
footprint and minimal external use, and a licence cannot deliver that: **GitHub's Terms of
Service grant every user the right to fork a public repository regardless of its licence.**
Access control is the only real mechanism.

## What breaks when it goes private

1. **`pm-check` checks out `cpheinrich/morpheus`** to build the CLI. Needs a PAT with `repo`
   scope in each consuming repo's Actions secrets.
2. **Reusable workflows** need Settings → Actions → Access set to allow other repositories.
3. **Actions minutes** come out of quota rather than being unlimited.

All three are one-time setup. None is hard.

## Approach

Flip visibility, add the PAT to Evo and Darwin, set the access policy, and verify Evo's CI
still passes. Reconsider the licence separately — once private, it matters much less, since
nobody can read the source without being granted access.

## Dropped

2026-07-29 — staying public. Chris wants to share Morpheus with close friends without minting
them a PAT, which is exactly the friction going private would reintroduce. The PolyForm
Noncommercial licence stays; it does not prevent forks, and that is now an accepted trade rather
than an open question.
