## Context

Evo's Gemini meeting-note pilot proved a deterministic schema, immutable Git reader, discovery
catalog, bounded search and completion-notice gate. Lakina is the second consumer. Copying the
pilot would freeze Evo's Darwin-owned topology into every project and leave the hard safety rules
to drift.

## Approach

Extract the deterministic capability into `morpheus-kit/meeting-intake` and expose it through the
CLI plus one loader-native shared skill. Keep collaborators, topics, canonical repository,
optional projection, timezone and notification authority in each project's config. Prove Evo's
existing projected topology and Lakina's same-repository/no-projection topology with invented
fixtures. Historical notes remain notes-only and cannot pass the notice gate.

## Acceptance

- Morpheus validates generalized discovery metadata, project topic policy and source identities.
- Immutable Git reads reject dirty checkout content, wrong origins, unsafe paths and empty corpora.
- Catalog/search/rendering have no Evo or Darwin business constants; projections are optional.
- The completion gate refuses historical imports, partial/stale delivery, duplicate/uncertain
  sends and profiles without explicit notification authority.
- A registered `meeting-intake` skill points every managed project at one shared guide that keeps
  factual notes separate from interpretation and actions.
- Focused tests exercise projected Evo and same-repository Lakina profiles, including Gmail,
  Calendar and Docs deduplication.
