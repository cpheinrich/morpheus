## Context

Since #362 (2026-10-08) `context brief` has opened about nineteen `wip/trunk-*` draft PRs and none
was ever adopted or merged. Evo looped: ten drafts in two days alternating between two
Xcode-generated diffs, about forty CI runs wasted. Every unadopted draft became a
`manager:needs-human` item; that queue went from 4 to 16.

## Approach

1. **Don't rescue IDE churn.** Classify the rescue commit's paths first. When every edit is
   per-user IDE state, a structure-preserving Xcode re-serialisation (pbxproj whitespace, comments,
   dictionary key order; scheme XML formatting) or content trunk already has, reset with a one-line
   report instead of pushing. One real edit keeps today's rescue.
2. **Dedupe by replay, and age out.** An open rescue holds the same edits when its change replayed
   onto HEAD yields exactly the new tree. The GitHub Manager sweep closes an untouched rescue draft
   after seven days, branch kept, and never escalates one.
3. **Clean up the seven open drafts** in Morpheus, Evo, Lakina and Kairos.

Done when the classifier, dedupe and age-out boundary are tested with fixture repos, the decision
record is superseded, and the open drafts are closed or reported with reasons.
