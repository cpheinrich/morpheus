## Context

Agents should hear about an over-complex function while they are writing it, not in a later audit.
Morpheus provides one pinned Lizard analyzer for every project, with a targeted check of changed
functions, a review-packet summary and a CI backstop. Evo enforces CCN ≤ 15 after its baseline
refactor; Lakina and Morpheus stay advisory. Mandate, acceptance and budget: the worklog.

## Approach

Explicit `morpheus complexity install` (hash-pinned Lizard archive plus venv, like codebase-memory),
`morpheus complexity check` over changed functions with policy from `morpheus.json`, a
`review prepare` section and a `complexity` job in `pr-check.yml`. Design under fresh review first.
