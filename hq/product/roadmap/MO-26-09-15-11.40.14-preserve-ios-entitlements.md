## Context

Evo TestFlight reports missing com.apple.developer.healthkit even though both build configurations declare it. The shared upload action compiles unsigned and exports without preserving app-declared entitlement claims. Its final checks cover identity only.

## Approach

Resolve the Release app entitlement file from the same build arguments used to archive, seed an ad-hoc signature before distribution export, and reject any exported IPA missing or changing a declared entitlement. No Apple development certificate or provisioning update is requested.
