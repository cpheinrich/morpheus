## Context

Evo established three useful marketing operating records independently: a website SEO strategy
grounded in OpenSEO and Search Console evidence, a privacy-bounded PostHog implementation backed by
a provider-neutral event contract, and a staged soft-launch plan. New Morpheus projects currently
receive the analytics contract and an SEO folder README, but not the briefs that tell an agent how
to turn those primitives into a project-specific measurement, search, and launch plan.

Without those records, every project has to rediscover the same boundaries: what evidence to gather,
which actions need approval, how website SEO differs from app-store discovery, and why analytics
events are defined before the provider SDK is installed.

## Approach

Add non-destructive initialization briefs for analytics, website SEO strategy, and launch planning
to company and personal projects. The launch plan covers a real website launch workflow and carries
an explicit placeholder for a future app launch; it does not imply that an app or store listing
exists. Keep Morpheus itself exempt as an internal project.

The initializer will write missing files without overwriting project-specific work. Doctor and
onboarding language will distinguish a scaffold from completed initialization. Architecture and
tests will document the layout, provider boundaries, privacy defaults, and external-action gates.
