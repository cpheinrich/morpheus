## Context

Chris gave owner decisions on 2026-10-30 after CD V2's first ten days: 17 failed deliveries and a
four-day staging stall. Checks that protect users block and the rest warn. Quota gates go. The
validation-campaign machinery is retired. Consumers use one Morpheus version, `main`. The ledger
should be as simple as the lead judges best. Production builds fresh `main` like staging, and CD
deploys the rules for the source it ships. Independent review gets five automatic rounds. The lead
chose to replace V2 with stateless per-slot delivery rather than slim it. Implementation starts in
parallel across both repositories, so the design and the stage list must be on record where
implementers and reviewers read them.

## Approach

Records and design only; no CD code, workflow, host or ledger change:

- architecture.md §18.2: replace the CD V2 block with the stateless target design. It covers the
  run's jobs, the classification of every V2 check (block, warn or delete), the ledger (run
  history plus receipt tags, and the migration off `state.json`), build numbers from App Store
  Connect, the single Morpheus reference, config read from `main`, the steward and watchdog, the
  stages with their parallel groups, rollback, and the removal estimate.
- `.agent/decisions.md`: Chris's decisions, the lead's stateless call, and what they supersede.
