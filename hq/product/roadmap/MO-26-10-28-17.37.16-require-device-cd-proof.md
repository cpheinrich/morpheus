## Context

Consumers can gate a workflow on physical-device tests, but the shared CD ledger currently retains only simulator/full-test evidence and upload admission has no configured hardware requirement.

## Approach

Extend the existing test evidence/proof with an opt-in, exactly configured physical-device inventory and execution binding. Validate before recording, before upload ownership changes, and before reusing accepted-upload proof. Preserve legacy configuration bytes, ordinary and typed post-resume identities, and unknown-upload reconciliation. Consumer runtime adoption remains separate.

## Acceptance

- Absent policy preserves existing normalized configuration and digest.
- Required device/host/profile and exact expected test IDs are checked against bound first-attempt results and hashed payload references.
- Missing, stale, substituted, incomplete, duplicate, skipped, retried or tampered evidence refuses without upload-owner/fence mutation.
- Accepted-upload reuse validates the required compact proof before Apple access.
- Existing immutable replay, holds, leases, typed post-resume provenance and unknown-upload behavior remain covered.
- A capability/config-round-trip seam allows consumer rejection of old policy-stripping runtimes.
