## Context

Lakina's independent review of its adoption of `morpheus-kit/section-links` (lakinacapital#593,
MO-26-10-28-23.32.51) found two defects in the component:

- **LKSL-001.** A shared link to a generated id inside a closed `<details>` left the reader at the
  top. `scrollIntoView` does not open it, though browser fragment navigation does.
- **LKSL-002.** In a single-column layout with a 16–20px margin, the icon was clamped to x=4 and
  covered the heading's first letter.

Fixing them in the kit before the remaining projects pin it means every project gets the fix.

## Approach

- `revealAncestors` opens every closed ancestor `<details>` before the scroll.
- `measure` keeps the icon wholly in the margin. It first shrinks the gap from 6px to 2px, then the
  icon down to 12px. It returns no icon where even that would overlap the text.

## Acceptance

- Opening a link to a generated id inside closed `<details>` opens them and scrolls to the heading.
- The icon never overlaps the heading text. At 860px and 600px in Lakina's single column it fits the
  margin, and with no room it is not shown.
