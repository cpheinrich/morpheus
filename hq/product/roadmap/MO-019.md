## Context

Codex reviewed the brand wizard ([#5](https://github.com/cpheinrich/morpheus/issues/5)) and found
a real bug: `brand init` wrote an empty `hq/brand/tokens.json` even when `visualSource` was set.
Running it on Darwin or Evo — both of which have significant existing design work — would have
created a **second canonical token system** beside the real one. Both files would look plausible,
which is why nobody would notice.

It also correctly identified that `architecture.md` named two owners for tokens: §3 said
`packages/shared/tokens/` was the DTCG source, §15.1a said `hq/brand/tokens.json` held primitives.
Ambiguous ownership is what lets an initializer duplicate a system.

## Shipped

**Generation is non-destructive.** No file is ever overwritten; anything present is skipped and
reported. No `tokens.json` at all when `visualSource` is set.

**`morpheus brand refresh`** re-runs the wizard with previous answers prefilled — enter keeps each
one. Answers persist to `hq/brand/answers.json`. The wizard now says so up front, because the
pressure to get a brand perfect on the first pass is what stops people writing one down at all.

**Token ownership is now unambiguous** — primitives in `hq/brand/tokens.json` (change on rebrand),
semantic mapping in `packages/shared/tokens/semantic.json` (change on redesign), generated output
never hand-edited. §15.1a marked authoritative and §3 corrected.

## Deliberately not built: `brand migrate`

Codex proposed a command with DTCG merging, conflict detection, dry-run, and atomic aborts. Its
*invariants* are right and are now enforced by the non-destructive writer. The *tooling* is
premature:

- There are exactly **two** projects to migrate, and both are being done by hand anyway.
- The hard part is judgment — which of two conflicting values is canonical depends on which
  surface ships — and a merger cannot do that.
- Extract on the second use. Two hand-migrations become the specification for the tool, the same
  way the Evo retrofit became the specification for `init`.

Revisit when a third project needs it.
