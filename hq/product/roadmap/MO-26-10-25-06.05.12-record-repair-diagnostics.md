## Context

A repair owner needs new diagnostic data after its latest native attempt has
failed. The normal failure recorder correctly requires an active attempt; making
that recorder accept failed attempts would also weaken execution ownership.
The existing repair remains the sole owner of continuation and delivery.

## Approach

Add `cd record --kind repair-diagnostic --evidence FILE` as an evidence-only
shared API. Strict JSON binds the exact work/task digest, current intent and
latest failed attempt's already-bound immutable execution identity. Require the
unique latest noncomplete repair, unchanged task, repairing undelivered intent
and unchanged source. Append only one deduplicated canonical diagnostic string
through the normal GitHubStore CAS/event path. Bound the untrusted diagnostic to
16 KiB. Check every new append and each fresh CAS callback. Verify exact saved
receipt/evidence on readback; a genuine replay retains its immutable note even
after normal owner progression, while reporting that the target is no longer current.

The API grants no instruction, authority or funding and changes no attempts,
tests, uploads, receipts, source lineage, clocks, holds, budgets, cursors,
leases or owner fences. Existing active-attempt guards remain strict. Refuse
unknown, stale, sibling, complete, delivered, retargeted and raced input.

Acceptance: synthetic whole-object preservation tests, exact dedupe/replay,
lost acknowledgment/collision/CAS completion refusal, identity/task/source
tampering, UTF-8 byte boundaries and unchanged normal record guards. Run focused
tests and targeted mutants, typecheck, compile and PM index before review.

Authority: source publication, one fresh independent high-risk review, CI and
protected merge under the delegated CD recovery mandate, after the lead agent's
exact packet/cost inspection and protected workflow-fix integration. The separately earmarked source maximum
is $5, with at most eight full and four metadata CI runs; this item does not
reserve or settle funds and grants no native/provider/model/runtime effects.
