## Context

Step 7 of `.claude/skills/launch-company/SKILL.md` says `vercel-deploy.yml` is the workflow "which
the scaffolded CI calls on merge to `main`". `init` scaffolds no such caller (`node-ci`,
`pm-check`, `pr-check` and, for consumer auth, `firebase-tests` only). An agent following the skill
may assume deploys are wired, merge, and see nothing deploy — or fall back to the local path the
step just ruled out. Raised as LC-F4 in the follow-up review of PR #209 after clearance, so it was
deferred rather than edited after the covered commit.

## Approach

Reword the sentence as something the launch must add: "add a project workflow that calls
`cpheinrich/morpheus/.github/workflows/vercel-deploy.yml@main` on push to `main` (init does not
scaffold it) with the three Vercel values as GitHub Actions secrets". Pin the new wording in the
launch-company assertions in `tests/voice.test.ts`. Small, low-risk review.
