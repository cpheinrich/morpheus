## Context

An enrolled worker lost its confirmed 60-second lease in a 74-second interruption while the tool was testing a QA patch. The workspace and output were retained, but the interrupted attempt needed operator replay.

## Approach

The service acknowledges a two-minute renewal grant. Supervisors use it without wall-clock assumptions and keep the existing one-minute fallback during rollout. Cancellation prevents new grants. The verified-cleanup requirement still governs slot release.

## Acceptance

- One confirmed new grant covers a 74-second transient interruption, with one invocation and ordinary terminal delivery.
- An older service keeps the existing confirmed cutoff. An unrecognized grant cannot launch or extend a worker.
- The service and runner agree on the duration. Cancellation remains authoritative and an expired or canceled attempt cannot renew.
