## Context

Chris explicitly authorized consecutive real internal TestFlight validation
deliveries without staging or production waiting periods, with main changes
merged between builds. Manual validation bypasses cadence today, but production
age is enforced at creation, attempt planning and repair adoption. Changing global
policy would also change configuration identity and weaken ordinary deployment.

## Approach

Add a source-age waiver only to explicitly requested validation intents, recording
the actual approval reference and request timestamp. Require an exact verified
main source, preserve the waiver immutably through repairs, and expose it in
status. Retain normal scheduling, source age, cursor and evidence gates.

## Acceptance

- Fresh production source can dispatch for an explicitly waived validation
  lineage, including a reviewed app repair, to the configured real internal group.
- Missing validation mode, missing exact source, unknown source or malformed
  approval metadata cannot create a waiver request.
- Waiver metadata cannot be added, changed or removed after intent creation.
- Ordinary and unwaived validation still enforce the exact 24-hour production
  boundary; full native and internal TestFlight completion requirements remain.
