## Context

Chris requested authenticated Paperclip access through his existing HQ, including the actual
board and continuous controller observations. The earlier transport delivered command receipts
but required a manually refreshed ID token and had no live board projection.

## Approach

Extend the accepted single-company callable transport with connector-only bounded publication,
live-human snapshot reads and a private token-renewing outbound daemon. Preserve existing Pool
admission, typed actions, uncertain-operation reconciliation and company binding. The consumer
UI, credentials, installation and user-visible acceptance are separately verified by the lead.

## Acceptance

- The existing HQ user can read actual bounded Paperclip board/agent/comment observations with
  visible age and partial-data flags, without widening human access.
- The outbound connector renews its Firebase identity and preserves existing typed-action,
  single-Pool and uncertain-outcome behavior across ordinary reconnect.
- Source delivery has focused tests, independent review and passing CI. Consumer enrollment,
  deployment, rendered UI and recovery evidence are recorded separately before claiming the pilot
  accepted; a source merge alone is not the user-visible result.
