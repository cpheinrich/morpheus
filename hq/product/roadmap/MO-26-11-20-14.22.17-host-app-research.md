## Context

Project teams need a shared way to inspect autonomous app studies, including captured states, controls, and the evidence behind their annotations.

## Approach

Publish a documented study contract, integrity-checked browser viewer, local validation command, and a small attributed Appeeky snapshot. Projects configure their own app catalog, permissions, and branding.
