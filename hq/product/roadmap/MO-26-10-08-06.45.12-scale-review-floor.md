## Context

The 30-second normal/high-risk floor in `src/review/local.ts` refused six clean, 21–30 s
independent reviews of the same five-line workflow `if:` change (evo#412, lakinacapital#509,
kairos#70). On a change that small the floor measures the diff, not the reviewer's diligence.

## Approach

Chris chose option A: waive the floor when the validator, reading Git over the reviewed range
(`base`..`covered`), finds a trivially small change — at most 20 non-test lines and 40 test lines,
every non-test file configuration, workflow or prose, worklogs and `hq/product/` not counted,
nothing binary. Named constants, runbook paragraph, decision entry, boundary tests.
