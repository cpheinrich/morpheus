## Context

The approved CD V2 controller must qualify an exact app source and claim upload before
contacting Apple. Shared iOS adapters currently select the workflow source and allocate
build numbers locally, without durable operation identity or trusted ledger hooks.

## Approach

Add exact source/execution inputs and actual-run metadata to the reusable test workflow.
Expose reserved build identity and trusted before/resource/after upload hooks in the
composite action, hash the verified IPA before claiming, preserve provisional resource
and accepted evidence, and permit the controller to own internal distribution.

Keep legacy callers compatible and test the real upload boundary with fake Apple/hooks.
No live upload or cutover occurs in this adapter PR. Independent review and main CI are
required before merge; the parent campaign owns operational delivery acceptance.
