## Context

Morpheus-managed sites need an authenticated way to collect visual feedback on their
deployed pages. Selected operators can also request a Factory build.

## Approach

Export an inline browser panel and shared bounded payload schema. Site adapters own
identity, data validation, persistence and the Factory queue. See
[the QA comments guide](../../../docs/runbooks/qa-comments.md#production-web-qa).
