## Context

Issue #560 reports legitimate real-timer observations of 2998–2999 ms failing the CLI cleanup-fuse assertion. The production timeout is already three seconds; millisecond wall-clock measurements do not establish an exact scheduling boundary.

## Approach

Allow 50 ms of measurement slack in the real-process lower bound, retaining the four-second upper bound and SIGTERM/SIGKILL sequence. Strengthen the existing fake-clock unit test at 2999 ms and 3000 ms so the exact production deadline remains tested deterministically. Do not change production timeouts or add dependencies.

## Acceptance

- Both compiled quota and worker lifecycle tests tolerate the reported 2998–2999 ms observation while retaining bounded cleanup.
- Fake-clock tests prove no SIGKILL at 2999 ms and SIGKILL at exactly 3000 ms.
