## Context

Zod 4.6.6 fixes the root production graph's high-severity GHSA-mrhx advisory, but Zod 4.5 also
began rejecting qualified ISO datetimes without seconds. Morpheus previously accepted values such
as `2026-11-02T12:34Z` and `2026-11-02T12:34+00:00`; silently narrowing that contract would reject
persisted PM, CD, review, and meeting-intake records during a security update.

## Approach

Upgrade the governed root graph to Zod 4.6.6, centralize the documented optional-seconds contract
as the explicit union Zod recommends, and add boundary tests through each affected schema owner.
Keep local unqualified/offset rules unchanged, regenerate committed declarations, and isolate the
blocked Dependabot PR as superseded rather than widening its exact-dependency scope.
