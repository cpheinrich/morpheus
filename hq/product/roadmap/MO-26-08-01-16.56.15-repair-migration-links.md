## Context

The Morpheus id migration shipped with **28 dangling markdown links**, and the generated index
added **77 more**. Both are my defect, and neither was caught before merge.

Two distinct causes:

1. **Markdown links were never repaired.** The migration repointed `roadmap:` frontmatter — a
   structured reference — but not `[MO-002](../roadmap/MO-26-07-28-002-reusable-github-workflows.md)`, which is equally a link by the
   rule already written down in `.agent/decisions.md`.
2. **The generated index still assembled `./<id>.md`.** Filenames stopped equalling ids in MO-057
   when items gained a slug, and `index-gen.ts` kept reconstructing a filename it was already
   handed. Three tables, every row.

## Why it was not caught

Morpheus has no test asserting relative links resolve. **Darwin does**, and its `web / web` job
failed on the darwin migration PR — which is the only reason this surfaced at all. The Morpheus
migration had already merged.

That is the lesson worth keeping: the repo with the weaker test suite shipped the defect, and the
repo with the stronger one found it.

## What changes

- `pm migrate-ids` repairs relative markdown links to renamed items, leaving **link text** alone —
  it is prose, and the old number still reads correctly
- absolute URLs are never rewritten: an archived link pinned to a branch records what that file was
  called on that branch, and "fixing" it breaks a working link to tidy a local one
- `index-gen.ts` links to `basename(item.path)` rather than reconstructing `<id>.md`
- Morpheus's own links are repaired

## Not in scope

19 links in `.agent/inbox-archive/` are broken and stay broken. Verified against the commit before
the migration: they were already broken, from archived inboxes moving out of `hq/inbox/` without
their relative paths being updated. Unrelated, and fixing them here would hide that.

## Test plan

Two new cases: links to renamed items are repaired while link text and un-renamed targets are
untouched, and an absolute URL to a renamed item is left alone. Verified against the real repo that
broken relative links fell from 105 to 19, and that the remaining 19 pre-date the migration.
