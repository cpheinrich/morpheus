## Context

Independent review finding F3 on PR #435 (MO-26-10-20-07.06.38) found that
`runWorker` captures its lease anchor before admission. A quota read may now
consume up to 60 seconds, reducing the existing margin between the 45-minute
guardian deadline and the `agentMinutes + 2` lease expiry. Login, claim, teardown
and completion transactions also consume that margin. A loaded timeout path can
therefore reach the completion fence after lease expiry; CAS refuses the stale
owner, but another worker may have already claimed. The finding is minor and
deferred here; no live runtime or lease was changed by the source repair.

## Approach

Anchor lease/start and ownership checks at a fresh clock inside each CAS claim
attempt after quota/login admission. Revalidate measured quota there and after
claim acknowledgment before launch. Require the returned owner/fence and strictly
more lease headroom than the configured agent duration, without renewal. Preserve
the existing fenced error cleanup,
agent/lease durations, quota TTL, meter and guardian. Focused synthetic worker/CAS
tests cover near-bound startup/login/read delays, exact expiry/reset boundaries,
conflict/replay/competing ownership and the configured guardian budget boundary.
No guardian worst-case cleanup guarantee is added. Runtime adoption remains a
separate quiet-host operational gate; this source task authorizes no service,
native worker, live lease/session, consumer or runtime mutation.
