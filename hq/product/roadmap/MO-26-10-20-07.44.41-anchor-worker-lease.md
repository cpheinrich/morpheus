## Context

Independent review finding F3 on PR #435 (MO-26-10-20-07.06.38) found that
`runWorker` captures its lease anchor before admission. A quota read may now
consume up to 60 seconds, reducing the existing margin between the 45-minute
guardian deadline and the `agentMinutes + 2` lease expiry. Login, claim, teardown
and completion transactions also consume that margin. A loaded timeout path can
therefore reach the completion fence after lease expiry; CAS refuses the stale
owner, but another worker may have already claimed. The finding is minor and
deferred here; no live runtime or lease was changed by the source repair.

## Approach

Evaluate anchoring the claim/lease after actual quota and login admission while
preserving the agent execution policy, ownership fences, metering and fail-closed
behavior. Add a fake-clock worker/CAS regression with near-bound quota startup
and final guardian timeout so lease margin is measured from the actual claim.
Use a separately claimed source task and independent review. Adoption in frozen
workers remains a separate quiet-host operational gate; this item authorizes no
service, worker, lease or runtime mutation.
