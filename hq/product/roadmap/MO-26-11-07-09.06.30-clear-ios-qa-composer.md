## Context

Enter saves an iOS QA pin but leaves the saved text in the editor, which makes it unclear whether the pin was saved.

## Approach

Close and clear the editor after Enter. Keep the pin available to reopen and send. Verify both original and edited saves, including reload before sending, in the browser test.
