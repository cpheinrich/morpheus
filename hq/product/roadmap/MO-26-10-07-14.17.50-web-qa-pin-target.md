## Context

Chris right-clicked the "Mixed" badge on a Lakina experiment card and the pin landed at the left of the card (2026-10-07). The card's title link is stretched over the whole card (a::after), so elementFromPoint returned the link, whose own box is only its text; the offset was clamped to its edge.

## Approach

Pick the first element in `elementsFromPoint` whose own box contains the click point; when none does, draw the pin at the clicked page point and keep the element for the agent. Verified on a separate test overlay (port 4399) against the same dev server: the pin anchors to the badge `strong` and is drawn at its centre.
