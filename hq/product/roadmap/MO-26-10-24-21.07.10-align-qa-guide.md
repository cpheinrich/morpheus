## Context

Issue #484: the printed `morpheus qa guide` still describes web Send-time whole-page PNGs after #483 introduced primary per-comment HTML context and placement viewport JPEGs. That can make responders map full-page fractions onto the wrong image.

## Approach

Align only the printed guide and its regression coverage/generated output with the existing reviewed schema/runbook: distinguish current web HTML/JPEG evidence, original viewport click/scroll and exact downscale mapping, scope and omissions/failure; preserve separate iOS PNG and legacy web whole-page instructions. No capture, preview or responder behavior changes.

## Acceptance

- Current web instructions use `comment.webContext` first and per-pin viewport JPEGs (1600 px cap, quality 0.5).
- Original viewport point, window/nested scroll and encoded per-axis scales map the JPEG correctly; full-page anchor fractions are not used on it.
- iOS placement PNG and legacy Send-time page-frame semantics remain explicit and separate.
- Focused tests, printed compiled-guide inspection, typecheck, compile, indexes, independent review and CI pass.
