## Context

User-facing Morpheus projects already receive analytics, website SEO, and launch briefs, but they
do not receive durable operating records for organic social channels. That leaves every project to
rediscover account ownership, platform-native content guidance, measurement, and the safety seam
between an AI-prepared draft and an external action.

The longer-term marketing system also needs an explicit home: a virtual AI CMO that continually
turns product analytics, search and store evidence, channel analytics, customer language, market
research, and competitor research into a prioritized organic growth portfolio.

## Approach

Add Instagram, LinkedIn, X, and Reddit directories to every company and personal project. Give each
one a project-owned README with public account fields, platform-specific creation and measurement
guidance based on current first-party sources, and a conservative automation boundary. An absent
account remains valid and visible as a setup reminder; existing records are never overwritten.

Expand the marketing root README and architecture into the virtual-CMO operating loop across SEO,
ASO, GEO/AI visibility, social, community, market/competitor research, launch work, and live
analytics. Extend doctor and initializer tests so new scaffolds include the four channel records,
internal projects remain exempt, and established channel work is preserved.

## Acceptance

- `morpheus init` creates `hq/marketing/{instagram,linkedin,x,reddit}/README.md` for company and
  personal projects, without creating them for internal projects or overwriting an existing file.
- Each channel record includes public account identity fields, platform-native guidance, meaningful
  outcome metrics, reviewed first-party sources, and explicit external-action/automation gates.
- `hq/marketing/README.md` describes a continuing evidence-to-experiment virtual-CMO loop across
  SEO, ASO, GEO/AI visibility, organic social, market and competitor research, and analytics.
- `morpheus doctor` reports a missing expected channel directory in user-facing projects.
- Typecheck, tests, build, generated artifacts, PM indexes, and whitespace validation pass.
