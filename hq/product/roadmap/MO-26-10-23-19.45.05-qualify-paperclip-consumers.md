## Context

Continuation of the original acceptance of
[MO-26-10-23-00.15.26](MO-26-10-23-00.15.26-qualify-paperclip-projects.md), explicitly
preserved when its first increment was narrowed to the authenticated bridge and two read-only
snapshot jobs. The accepted two-host pilot does not qualify consumer repository delivery.
Its original reverse tunnel reached an implicit-admin local-trusted board; manual wakeup keys
did not deduplicate; the actual custom CLI hot restart produced an artifact and then interrupted.
See `docs/paperclip-pilot-results.md` and `docs/paperclip-integration.md`.

## Approach

Qualify authenticated/private deployment and actual scoped worker authority before widening
the trust boundary. Use existing Morpheus claims/worktrees/review/CI and the existing iOS VM
pool; build neither another queue nor another simulator manager. Keep provider authentication
and saved sessions on their registered hosts. Chris is handling subscription-sharing policy;
additional account/owner enrollment and remote provider inference remain deferred and require
their own authority. Read-only deterministic tools on Zoe do not establish provider eligibility.

## Preserved acceptance

- Deliver one actual low-risk consumer repository task on each authorized host through its
  normal claim, isolated worktree, focused checks, fresh independent review, required CI and
  protected merge, with exact caller-visible acceptance evidence. Read-only snapshot jobs do
  not satisfy this criterion.
- Prove repeatable new-host setup with native owner sign-in and host/account/workspace identity
  binding, without copying provider credentials or inventing new subscriptions. Test actual
  worker token access to its intended issue and denial of peer-agent, runtime, secret,
  agent-creation and wrong-project actions. Prove the real executor's filesystem/network/tool
  boundary, including MCP/plugin or computer-use escape surfaces.
- Demonstrate safe interruption before and after meaningful effects. Reconcile exact task/run,
  process termination, artifact and external-effect outcomes before continuation; show no
  duplicate external effect or unbudgeted inference. Logical retry rows are distinct from
  actual provider launches. Prove or explicitly constrain upstream hot-restart behavior;
  pause/drain maintenance remains required until seamless adoption is established.
- Integrate simulator/Xcode/CI work with the existing resource ownership and VM pool before
  claiming those capabilities. Avoid simultaneous source authors, global simulator mutation or
  a second resource manager. A non-iOS snapshot inspection proves none of this.
- Collect prospective task metrics defined in the pilot design, including accepted outcomes
  per measured allowance consumed. Keep model routing experimental until that evidence exists;
  record requested model/effort and observed model/effort separately, retaining unknown served
  attribution when the native protocol does not expose it. Cross-account failover and mid-task
  migration require their own exact continuation/session evidence.

This backlog item authorizes no new paid service, account, production release, additional host,
API-credit fallback or reset. Source qualification and a future operational rollout remain
separately reviewable. No requirement is waived by the increment split.
