## Context

A live Fleet worker can exit normally and record completed work while one last child delays the initial guardian cleanup result. The supervisor could verify termination later but discarded the completed structured output.

## Approach

Return the original completed result and declared artifacts after late cleanup for the same still-supervised attempt and current lease. Bind the checkout and admission before reading artifacts and use the existing upload and verified settlement. Keep cleanup-only recovery for restart, cancellation and expired authority.

## Acceptance

- Late proof for a live exact attempt preserves completed output without relaunch.
- Missing proof, foreign bindings, lost authority and unreadable results cannot publish artifacts.
- Verified cleanup still settles and frees the slot when the retained result cannot be restored.
