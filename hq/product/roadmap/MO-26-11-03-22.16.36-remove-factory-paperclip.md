## Context

Chris confirmed Paperclip is unconfigured and not in use. The Factory plugin still teaches agents to route remote-worker requests through Paperclip, obscuring the active gateway worker contract.

## Approach

Remove remote-pool guidance and MCP/CLI exposure from the current agent-facing Factory plugin. Preserve the six provider-neutral Factory tools, gateway routing/configuration and legacy backend modules used by separate integrations. Verify discovery and rejected obsolete entry points with local synthetic fixtures. Broader Paperclip integration deletion is out of scope.
