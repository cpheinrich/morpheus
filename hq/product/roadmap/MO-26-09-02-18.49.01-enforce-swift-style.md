## Context

Native builds currently compile and test Swift but do not mechanically enforce a consistent style.
Every iOS consumer should be able to adopt the formatter shipped with its selected Xcode toolchain
without copying workflow logic or adding a third-party install.

## Approach

Add an opt-in strict `swift-format` gate to the reusable iOS workflow. Validate the caller-owned
configuration, lint Swift files changed by the checked-out commit, document the incremental
adoption contract, and cover the reusable-workflow boundary with regression tests.
