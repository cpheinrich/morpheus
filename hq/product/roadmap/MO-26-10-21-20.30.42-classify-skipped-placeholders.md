## Context

The controller's no-start classifier requires `started_at` to be null. Retained
GitHub job rows show that terminal jobs that never reached a runner do not report
null. Skipped rows, and an ordinary iOS job cancelled while queued, report
`started_at` equal to `created_at`. They also report no runner, no runner group and
no steps. The controller therefore cannot classify a run whose cancelled-before-assignment
native job sits beside real skipped downstream rows. It sends such a run to repair
instead of the designed hosted fallback. The controller's own 15-minute admission
cancellation produces this shape.

## Approach

Recognize one terminal placeholder shape and leave every other rule unchanged.
The shape is a completed skipped or cancelled row with a completion time, `started_at`
string-identical to `created_at`, null or zero/empty runner and runner-group fields,
and zero steps. The strict null-start rule still governs queued rows, and the job
schema check does not change. Census completeness, run linkage, executor, preflight,
race and ownership guards also stay as they are. This is source and offline
validation only. Runtime adoption is separate reviewed work, as are the drill
target's cancellation shape and the real queued-row shape. No provider call, dispatch
or cancellation is part of this task. The design and boundary matrix are in the
acceptance file.
