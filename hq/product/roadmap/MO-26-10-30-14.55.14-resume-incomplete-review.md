## Context

Chris confirmed that requiring a separate human approval before the original agent reviewer can
finish an incomplete review was unintentional. The current validator requires it even when the
ordinary three-turn budget still has a turn available, which leaves otherwise ready PRs waiting.

## Approach

Allow the same reviewer to resume an incomplete turn within the ordinary cap without
`humanAuthorization`. Preserve the incomplete verdict and all timing, budget, identity and
clearance checks. Update instructions, prompt, runbook, architecture and regression tests. Keep
human authorization for extra substantive turns beyond the cap and turns after finalization.
