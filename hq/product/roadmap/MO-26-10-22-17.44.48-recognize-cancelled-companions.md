## Context

A completed no-admission run can contain cancelled, never-assigned Ubuntu qualification dependents after successful preflight. The controller currently mistakes this complete census for unproven native execution and queues repair instead of its normal hosted successor.

## Approach

Keep exact-attempt/ownership/deadline protections. Add a narrow cancelled-Ubuntu companion predicate with explicit no runner/group/steps and successful executed preflight evidence; qualify the retained five-job shape and negative/racing cases. See `qa/acceptance/MO-26-10-22-17.44.48.md`. Source review/CI and coherent consumer runtime adoption remain separately required.
