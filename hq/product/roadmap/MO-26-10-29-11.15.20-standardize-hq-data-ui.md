## Context

Lakina's 14-column EXP-031 table reproduced an integrated control regression, and all three audited
HQs repeat table, chart, tooltip, axis and accessibility behavior locally. A shared `DataTable`
exists in name but does not own its complete scroll, control, responsive and accessibility boundary;
chart frames and exact-data fallbacks are similarly inconsistent.

## Approach

Define and implement the `DataTable` and `ChartFrame` contracts in
`docs/runbooks/hq-product-design.md`. Canary the real EXP-031 comparison at 1920, 1280 and 390px,
including filter visibility, help controls, column priority, overflow cues, keyboard access and all
empty/error/missing-value states. Standardize conventional Recharts frames, legends, tooltips,
reduced motion, accessible summaries and exact-value fallbacks while leaving domain transforms and
bespoke explanatory SVG geometry project-owned. Adopt one second-project table and chart before
wide migration.
