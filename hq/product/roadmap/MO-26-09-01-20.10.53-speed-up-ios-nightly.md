## Context

Evo's pre-TestFlight suite spends almost all of its test time in serial UI flows. The suite is
valuable and should remain intact, but its release caller needs more macOS compute and an explicit
simulator-worker ceiling. The reusable workflow filename also sorts away from the other iOS
workflows, making the native workflow family harder to scan.

## Approach

Rename the reusable workflow to `ios-nightly-build.yml`, add a validated maximum parallel test
worker input to `ios-ci.yml`, forward that contract through the nightly release workflow, and
update every downstream caller. Evo will use `macos-26-xlarge`, enable parallel testing, and cap
Xcode at six simulator workers while retaining the separately gated archive/upload job.
