## Context

Repeated native repair checkpoints can be caused by the quota caller's
10-second whole-chain timer while a configured shim performs version and two
configuration-reader lifecycles before the actual account RPC. A stale or unknown
quota result must still prevent admission and stop metered work.

## Approach

Allow one fresh account read 60 seconds total and its final RPC 10 seconds within
that total, drain stderr and retain only fixed phase/elapsed diagnostics. Use a
separate bounded reader-only foreground supervisor, leaving model-guardian
defaults unchanged. Validate quota at actual completion and retain ordinary
eligibility, telemetry TTL, nonoverlap, CAS ownership and checkpoint rules.

Focused offline protocol/process fixtures cover startup and RPC boundaries,
cleanup, ownership separation and fail-closed metering. Runtime adoption is
explicitly separate: no consumer/host/service/config/ledger/session mutation.
