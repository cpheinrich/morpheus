## Context

Stage M1 of the CD simplification (architecture.md §18.2 "Stateless iOS delivery"; decision
2026-10-30). Evo is CD V2's only consumer. Once Evo's stateless delivery (`ios-delivery.yml`,
EV-26-10-30-15.25.08) is the only scheduler and V2's workflows and host agents are retired (Evo
stage S4, cutover), V2's controller has no caller, and keeping it costs every reader. Delete it
rather than slim it.

Depends on: Evo S4 merged and its cutover acceptance met (one scheduled staging and one scheduled
production delivery through V3). Runs in parallel with Evo S5 (deleting V2 from Evo); the two
touch different repositories. Evo's retired V2 workflows pin old Morpheus commits, so a rollback to
V2 still has its code after this merges.

## Approach

Delete, do not refactor:

- `src/cd/` (all, about 3,540 lines including `host/macos` templates and the Python quota reader
  and guarded runner), `tests/cd/` (about 7,500 lines), `dist/cd/` (regenerated by
  `pnpm compile`), the `./cd` export in `package.json`, the `morpheus cd` command in
  `src/cli/run.ts` and `src/cli/help.ts`.
- Docs: `docs/cd-pipeline-v2.md`, `-rca.md`, `-research.md`, `-validation.md`,
  `docs/cd-v2-controller.md`, `docs/cd-v2-host-macos.md`, `docs/cd-v2-claude-executor.md`,
  `docs/cd-source-finance.md`, `docs/runbooks/cd-repair-control.md`, and their `docs/README.md`
  rows. Keep a one-paragraph history pointer in architecture.md (already written by S0).
- `.github/actions/ios-testflight-upload`: remove the V2-only inputs (`reserved-build-number`,
  `before-upload-script`, `after-upload-script`, `distribution-enabled`) and `upload-effects.sh`
  after checking no registered consumer passes them. **Keep** `artifact-stage/` and
  `docs/artifact-snapshots.md`: Morpheus ios-ci uses them for every CD execution id, V3's included.
- Update mentions in `src/cto/skill.ts` and the devops-engineer and agentic-cto `provenance.md`
  references (both mirrors) so they describe stateless delivery.

Acceptance: `pnpm typecheck`, focused tests for the CLI, the upload action and skills, `pnpm compile`
and the full suite once (shared CLI changes); then the next scheduled Evo V3 delivery after merge
succeeds against the changed upload action at `@main`. Rollback: revert the PR.
