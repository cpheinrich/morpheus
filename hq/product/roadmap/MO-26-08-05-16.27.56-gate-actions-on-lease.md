## Context

[MO-26-08-05-12.24.47](MO-26-08-05-12.24.47-enforce-context-freshness.md)
landed the freshness *policy* — receipts, five-minute leases, a fail-closed
`requireFresh`. Nothing calls it. Until something does, a session can still
start without loading project state and run for hours past a change another
agent pushed; the policy just means the answer is now computable.

This item is the wiring, split out so the policy could merge without the board
recording enforcement that does not exist.

## Approach

- `morpheus context refresh | check | status`, and session start/resume that
  takes a receipt before PM work can begin.
- A remote-drift watcher on the lease term, and pre-commit/pre-push hooks plus
  gates on PM, deployment, data, and broker actions.
- An **offline exception**: an explicit mode that may work locally but cannot
  push or operate external controls. Today `requireFresh` throws on `unknown`
  unconditionally, so offline work is blocked rather than contained.
- PM/PR status that distinguishes claimed, context-current, and
  substantive-started, with CI validating the PR's receipt against the pushed
  SHA.
- Codex app-server (steering/interruption, reaching `SessionAdapter.interrupt`,
  which the policy declares and nothing yet calls) and Claude lifecycle/tool
  hook adapters, with contract tests, sharing one lease format and writing no
  raw session data.
- Initializer, registry, and managed-project rollout with adoption reporting.

## Acceptance

1. A stale, expired, or unknown lease blocks commit, push, and governed
   Morpheus actions.
2. Offline is an explicit, named exception that permits local work and refuses
   anything that leaves the machine.
3. Session start/resume refreshes before PM work begins, and drift inside a
   session names the smallest relevant canonical delta.
4. Codex and Claude adapters have contract tests and never write raw session
   data.
5. CI validates the PR's receipt/progress semantics against the pushed SHA,
   and generated projects receive hooks, policy, and adoption reporting.
