## Context

CI run 40189494065 (PR #505, source 130a6075ea78aff69426b86d72d25b664d14d70b) caught a pre-existing race: a legacy-storage cleanup retry replaces the successful Queued confirmation. The earlier same-source browser suite passed, so a deterministic regression must wait for cleanup after acknowledgment.

## Approach

Keep the primary action text/kind separately from durable draft warnings. Refresh warnings without replacing that action, and restore its normal styling when the warning clears. Verify failed cleanup after acknowledgment, one POST, route warning clearance, and no pin replay after reload in Chromium and WebKit; independently review final captures before normal protected merge.
