## Context

Darwin issue #386 reports that an address already in the HQ allowlist receives a 403 on its
first Google sign-in because the generated session route expects a preexisting Firebase role
claim. The one-time `access sync` path sees only users that already exist. Darwin repaired its
own app; Morpheus still generates the broken shared route.

## Approach

Generate a server-side lookup from the deployed manifest, reconcile the role claim only for a
verified Google sign-in, refresh the client's ID token before minting the cookie, and clear a
removed user's stale claim on the next sign-in. Keep `access sync` as the prompt bulk
revocation path. Test first sign-in, unlisted and stale accounts, admin precedence, and retry.
