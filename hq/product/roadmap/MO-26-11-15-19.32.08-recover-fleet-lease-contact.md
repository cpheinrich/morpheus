## Context

A transient control plane call could stop a live Fleet worker before its already renewed lease expired. It discarded otherwise viable work and left the next tick recovering incomplete output.

## Approach

Distinguish transient transport and availability errors from authority loss. Retry only inside the last confirmed lease, using its conservative monotonic deadline. Bound the whole control request and leave ownership, cleanup and publication checks with the existing protocol.

## Acceptance

- Transient inspect and renew errors allow the same active worker to continue without relaunch.
- A stalled request cannot extend the last confirmed lease.
- Explicit denial, cancellation and expiry still stop execution, with no stale result accepted.
- Verify the existing enrolled runner after protected delivery.
