## Context

The two-host artifact/decision pilot in MO-26-10-22-23.16.58 is accepted, but consumer
repository work is deliberately not enabled. Its reverse tunnel reaches a local-trusted board;
the real custom CLI hot restart produced an artifact then interrupted, with upstream process-loss
recovery eventually detecting it. Manual wakeup keys did not deduplicate. These observed limits
must be solved before broader autonomous dispatch. See `docs/paperclip-pilot-results.md`.

## Approach

Qualify upstream authenticated private deployment and scoped worker access before widening
the trust boundary. Integrate with existing Morpheus claims/worktrees/review/CI and the existing
iOS VM pool; do not build a second queue or simulator manager. Prove interrupted-task
reconciliation from exact action outcomes before continuation, and account identity grouping
without copying provider logins. Resolve or explicitly constrain upstream hot-restart behavior.

Acceptance: one real low-risk repository task per host through protected delivery, authenticated
access isolation probes, repeatable new-host setup with owner sign-in, safe interruption with no
duplicate external effect, and prospective task metrics defined in the pilot design. Keep model
routing experimental until acceptance per allowance consumed is measured; record exact requested
and observed model/effort separately. This is backlog qualification, not permission for new paid
services, new accounts, production releases or additional hosts.
