## Context

Live Darwin and Evo website feedback records a pin and deployment revision. It needs a private viewport image and bounded browser and page diagnostics that describe the placement, including the input that placed the pin.

## Approach

Extend the shared production QA panel and payload using the installed viewport rasterizer. Freeze evidence when the pin is placed, mask sensitive content before rendering, and keep the evidence with the draft and identical retries. Darwin and Evo will enable capture and commit the batch and each pin's evidence together. Keep the batch readable without loading the screenshots and preserve previous submissions.

## Acceptance

- A supported Chromium or WebKit client can place multiple pins on distinguishable, scrolled views and read back each matching screenshot, capture timestamp, input, browser family and available version, viewport, page context, and safe runtime metadata.
- Private regions, form values, QA controls and sensitive/unsupported media are redacted or omitted. The request carries no page markup, raw logs, credentials or request bodies.
- A marked capture failure is visible before sending. Evidence survives draft reload and retry. Legacy clients and records remain readable. Retrying a batch with changed evidence conflicts.
- Verify the full flow using synthetic content on Darwin and Evo with authorized readback of the saved JPEG and diagnostics.
