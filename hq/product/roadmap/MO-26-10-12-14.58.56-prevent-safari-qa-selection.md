## Context

Safari's Live Text recognizes the text inside the iOS simulator's MJPEG image.
Dragging to scroll can select that text, leaving blue selection bars over the app.
The native simulator capture is clean; the artifact is in the QA browser overlay.

## Approach

Make the image display-only for pointer hit testing and stop browser selection on
the stage while forwarding the same pointer sequence to the simulator. Verify
the drag behavior and a native-vs-Safari capture without interrupting an active
QA session.
