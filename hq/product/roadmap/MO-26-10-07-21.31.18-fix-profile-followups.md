## Context

Two follow-ups from the independent review of `morpheus profile` (#355):

1. Time spent waiting on a human — a permission prompt or an `AskUserQuestion` answer — lands in
   phase `other` and counts as active agent time whenever it is under the four-hour cap, inflating
   both the `other` bucket and active time.
2. `--since` accepts impossible calendar dates such as `2026-02-30`, which `Date` silently rolls
   over to March 2.

## Approach

Classify `AskUserQuestion` calls and tool calls whose result records a permission decision into a
distinct `human-wait` phase, and exclude `human-wait` spans from active time. Validate `--since`
strictly by round-tripping the parsed date, with tests at the month-end boundary.
