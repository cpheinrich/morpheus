## Context

A connected, enrolled physical iPhone can appear offline when unrelated CoreDevice rows make the inventory exceed the reporter’s subprocess output limit.

## Approach

Filter both passive CoreDevice inventory reads to the exact enrolled identifier. Keep the bounded command, exact identity validation, and lease bracketing. Verify the focused reporting tests and roll out the reviewed reporter to the affected host.
