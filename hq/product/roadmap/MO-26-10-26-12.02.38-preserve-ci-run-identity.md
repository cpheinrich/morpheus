## Context

Issue #492 retained a failed required CI job and a successful metadata job on the same PR head. Name-only deduplication hid the failure and reported green despite protection remaining blocked.

## Approach

Keep workflow and check-suite identities in each rollup row. Distinct workflow IDs remain separate regardless of required status or display name; only attempts of the same named job in the same workflow supersede each other. External checks retain suite identity. Missing suite identity refuses the receipt. Preserve commit-status and head-change behavior.

## Acceptance

- Both default and required-only waits report the failed sibling and its job URL.
- A later attempt in the same workflow replaces its prior failure.
- Same-named workflows with distinct IDs and legacy statuses cannot hide each other.
- Existing head-change and unregistered-workflow regressions continue to pass.
