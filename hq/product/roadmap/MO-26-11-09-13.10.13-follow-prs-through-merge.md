## Context

The merge runbook let authors end their session while auto-merge was queued. A failing required check could leave the PR waiting without its author.

## Approach

Align Morpheus's instructions, project scaffold and [merge runbook](../../../docs/runbooks/pm-workflow.md#merging-the-board-and-the-worklog). The author follows required CI, fixes or retries failures, and verifies the merge or records a hard blocker. GitHub Manager remains the backstop.
