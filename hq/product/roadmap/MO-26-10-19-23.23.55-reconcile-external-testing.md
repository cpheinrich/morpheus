## Context

An opted-in consumer must continue distributing each internally delivered build
to external testers, including Beta App Review, without making review delays or
provider errors revoke internal acceptance. Existing consumers must retain their
configuration identity and behavior when they do not declare external groups.

## Approach

Add optional external group identities, exact-build provider readback and separate
post-delivery status. Persist app/build review operation claims before provider
writes, preserve uncertain outcomes, and keep receipt, native/upload history,
ordinary/internal group cursors and budgets unchanged.

## Acceptance

- No opt-in means no external effects or configuration digest change.
- Exact external build state plus membership in every declared external group is
  required for availability; sibling review does not qualify.
- Review claims deduplicate concurrent invocations and groups, preserve unknown
  outcomes, and bound explicit conflict retries/submission admission.
- Internal delivery remains immutable under external review, rejection and error;
  historical isolated binaries, pauses and observe mode do not distribute externally.
- Focused state/provider/controller tests, independent review and full CI pass.

Source implementation only. Consumer migration, runtime rollout and live delivery
acceptance are owned by separate tasks; this item creates no provider groups or
testers and does not mutate a live ledger or service.
