## Context

Ordinary internal TestFlight delivery must keep priority while a separately authenticated, uncounted recovery-acceptance obligation waits on an uncertain provider result. Current timeout/repair paths can retire or replace that obligation, losing same-build recovery evidence; optional scheduling and shared group ownership also need explicit ordering. A bounded structural design has received independent review and its four concrete amendments are incorporated. This item implements source only; it does not select a live target, fault or runtime operation.

## Approach

Extend the existing CD state/controller/Store boundaries rather than adding another scheduler or operations writer. Use closed append-only origin/yield/execution-release records; protect the same active unknown optional upload across timeout, diagnosis, repair, retry and retarget paths; preserve exact existing-operation receipts and readback. Add ordinary-demand guards with a fresh clock inside every CAS retry. Keep ordinary group ownership unchanged while a narrow additive membership obligation uses one stable effect owner and read-only UNKNOWN reconciliation.

## Acceptance

- Preserve ordinary delivery behavior, production age, source and receipt cursors, original campaign records, old retired-uncertain observation-only behavior and existing accepted-upload capacity release. Ordinary validation=false builds are never enrolled in acceptance faults.
- Prove immutable authority records and exact guards across every effect-authorizing writer, including stale direct callers and late hooks, without rejecting legitimate matching receipts.
- Execute meaningful hermetic cases for zero-effect yield races, multi-poll same-build recovery beyond timeout, concurrent and lost-response effect claims, additive group ordering and time-only CAS threshold/expiry races. The reviewed design supplies28 falsifiable specifications, not executed tests.
- Keep the original campaign restrictions explicit; no new same-channel exemption, universal zero-delay claim, manufactured app input, source-age waiver or financial-hold admission gate.
- Document source behavior and limits, use independent exact-source review, required CI and protected merge. Runtime adoption, live journal/target selection and provider/fault effects remain separate work and are not authorized by this source item.
