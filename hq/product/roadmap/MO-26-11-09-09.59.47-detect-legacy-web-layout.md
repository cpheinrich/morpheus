## Context

Issue #433: established Next.js sites under `web/` are reported absent, hiding their HQ and inviting a second scaffold.

## Approach

Extend the existing survey to recognize `web/app` plus a Next.js package, preserving canonical/root precedence. Verify survey results and in-place scaffolding with fixtures; verify the real consumer read-only.

## Acceptance

- Legacy `web/` app and HQ are detected.
- Scaffolding preserves its existing HQ and never creates a second app.
- Empty, malformed and unrelated directories do not become Next.js apps.
- Existing canonical and root layouts keep precedence.
