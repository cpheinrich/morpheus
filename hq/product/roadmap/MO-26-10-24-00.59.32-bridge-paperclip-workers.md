## Context

Chris asked: “build this out using our existing bridge and Paperclip. Test delegating
from my Codex app to remote Claude and Codex workers, including streaming logs,
follow-ups, questions, and reconnecting.” The Codex conversation remains the coordinator;
Paperclip owns execution and history; Git remains the delivery authority.

## Approach

Extend the optional personal bridge, with provider-neutral pool operations. Reuse Paperclip's
queue/logs and the bridge's native provider protocols, question policy and process guardian.
Compare its native Runner before adding transport; never copy provider auth to another host.
See [the design](../../../docs/paperclip-interactive-bridge.md) and the worklog mandate.

Live Claude on Zoe's mini is pending native sign-in. Chris said he cannot sign in now and
instructed us to continue without it. Implement both providers and distinguish synthetic
transport evidence from actual provider acceptance. This does not waive live Claude acceptance.
