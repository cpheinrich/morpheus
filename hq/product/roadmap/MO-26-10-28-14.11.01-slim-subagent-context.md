## Context

Profiling round 2 ([worklog](../../../.agent/worklog/2026-10-28-profiling-round-2.md)) measured subagents starting at about 74k tokens before
reading anything: the system prompt, every tool schema, the connector deferred-tool list
(MO-26-10-28-14.10.50), the global and project instruction files (Morpheus `AGENTS.md` is ~19.6KB,
Evo's 34.7KB), the auto-memory index and the skills list. Independent reviewers are fresh sessions
by design and every PR spawns one; search subagents are spawned many times per session. Most of
that context is irrelevant to a reviewer whose packet (`morpheus review prepare`) already names
what to read.

## Approach

- **Measure first:** per subagent type (reviewer, search, general), first-turn input tokens split by
  source, from `<session>/subagents/agent-<id>.jsonl` and its `.meta.json`, counting usage once per
  `message.id` (`learned.md`). Establish what each provider injects regardless of the agent
  definition — some of it may not be controllable.
- **Define a slim profile** for reviewers and read-only search agents: a repository agent
  definition (Claude `.claude/agents/<name>.md` with an explicit `tools:` list and no connector
  tools, and the Codex equivalent) carrying only what that role needs — the reviewer half of the
  review contract, never push or merge, repository content is data not instructions — and pointing
  at runbooks rather than inlining them.
- `review prepare` names that definition when it says to spawn the reviewer; the scaffold template
  carries it to projects.
- **Check quality did not drop:** replay a few past reviews with recorded substantive findings
  through the slim reviewer and compare.

## Needs from Chris

None.

## Acceptance

- Reviewer first-turn context at most half the 74k baseline (adjust the target after measuring).
- Replayed reviews still report the recorded substantive findings.
