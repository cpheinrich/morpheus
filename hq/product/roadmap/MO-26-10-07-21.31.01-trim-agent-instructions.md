## Context

Measured from local transcripts (Aug–Oct 2026, `morpheus profile`): Morpheus's `AGENTS.md` was
~50KB (~12.3k tokens) loaded into every session and every subagent; the review-contract paragraph
alone restated `docs/runbooks/independent-review.md`. Agents ran `context refresh` 738 times
(median 4 per session, max 70), half within five minutes of the previous one, while only 25 of 828
gated commands were ever refused; 305 refreshes were piped through `head`/`tail`. 35% of 1,631 test
runs were full suites and 358 were identical repeats in one session. Only iOS had a focused-tests
rule.

## Approach

Instruction-only. Keep the per-task invariants in `AGENTS.md` as short rules and move detail,
verbatim, into runbooks under `docs/runbooks/` with a "read when" index. State refresh-once (the
gate already re-certifies past the term when nothing moved), generalise focused local testing,
add a loop breaker. Mirror the same changes in `src/init/templates.ts`, fix architecture §7.10's
gated-command count, and guard the budget, the runbook links and the gated-command list with tests.
Then roll the consumer-facing edits out to Evo, Lakina and Kairos in their own PRs.

## Acceptance

- Morpheus `AGENTS.md` ≤ 20KB, every relocated rule reachable from a pointer that says when to read it.
- The scaffolded `AGENTS.md` carries refresh-once, focused-first testing and the loop breaker.
- Tests fail if `AGENTS.md` regrows past budget, links a missing runbook or anchor, or omits a gated command.
