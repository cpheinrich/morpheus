## Context

The operator approved a read-only internal infrastructure dashboard with reusable host
reporting and a company-owned private presentation. Existing desktop inspection is scoped
to a root conversation; it is not a fleet census and can perform recovery mutations.
Reporting must cover desktop and unattended jobs without controlling either.

## Approach

First bounded stage: claim the supplied isolated checkout, verify real observation sources
and existing access, and prepare a concrete design for independent critique. Implementation
and deployment follow that critique under the existing authorization and review gates.

Proposed ownership: optional Factory plugin owns a strictly read-only fleet projection,
host metrics reporter and explicit launchd enrollment. Company-specific ingestion, identity
registration, storage and UI stay in the consuming repository. Local configuration remains
operational authority; the reporting projection cannot schedule, restart or grant access.

Acceptance: real registered hosts and active desktop/unattended workers; shared account quota
counted once; source-specific timestamps, unknown/failure/stale states; no prompt/log copying;
bounded latest state; authenticated scoped transport; restart/order/source-failure tests;
focused checks, complexity inspection, independent review and green protected CI before release.
Consumer acceptance also requires actual admin-only deployed readback and health observations.
