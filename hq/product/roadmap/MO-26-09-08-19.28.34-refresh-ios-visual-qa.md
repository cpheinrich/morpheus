## Context

Nightly visual QA needs a current, reviewable screenshot of every full-screen app destination in a persistent draft PR.

## Approach

Morpheus publishes a two-column gallery from exact-run XCTest attachments. Apps own synthetic screen inventories and captures. A separate completion observer cannot gate TestFlight; refreshes retain PR discussion and explicitly identify missing screens.
