## Context

Some first-party functions had grown past the point where a reader can hold every branch. The
analyzer measured 231 of them over cyclomatic complexity 15 at base 1d849394: 219 runtime rows, 7
executable test-support implementations and 5 embedded browser-script functions. A hard complexity
gate cannot be enabled until that baseline is at zero.

## Approach

Extract the real steps of each function family without changing behaviour. Prove each change with
focused tests and an old-vs-new differential. Re-scan with the canonical runner. Ship as a held WIP
draft; the hard gate stays off until a separate decision.
