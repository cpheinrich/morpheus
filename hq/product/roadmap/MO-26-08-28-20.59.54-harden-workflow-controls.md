## Context

The weekly audit reproduced twelve consecutive scheduled-workflow startup failures and found that
most reusable workflows trusted mutable third-party action tags. Both affect repository controls
and deserve an isolated draft rather than being folded into the routine audit PR.

## Approach

Give the scheduled caller the read permissions required by its nested reusable workflow, pin every
external action to an immutable commit, and test both the permission envelope and the pinning
policy. Leave the PR in draft until a dispatch on the branch proves the scheduler starts normally.
