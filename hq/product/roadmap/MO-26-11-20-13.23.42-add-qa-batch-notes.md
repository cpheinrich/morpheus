## Context

QA submitters need to give guidance that applies to every pin in a batch.

## Approach

Add optional Notes above the comment list in the simulator, local web and live web panels. Preserve drafts, send the field with the batch, and expose it to interactive and serial QA responders. Older batches remain readable. Live sites adopt the shared package and persist the field in their adapters.
