## Context

A configured existing external TestFlight group may intentionally have no testers. The shared controller currently treats that expected postreceipt condition as an invariant error, even though internal delivery is complete. External waiting must remain visible without fabricating external availability or changing tester membership.

## Approach

Keep exact receipt/app/group/type/automatic-distribution guards. Distinguish valid empty tester lists from malformed tester identities; after existing blocked/unknown build-state classification, record pending/wait-external-testers and the ordinary poll deadline, then return before any provider mutation. Fresh populated readback resumes the existing once-only distribution/review flow. No config, schema, store, internal receipt, cursor, budget, agent or upload change.

Local Phase A only: focused regression/mutation tests, docs, compile/index and a local commit. Source publication, fresh independent review, CI, merge and coherent consumer adoption require the separate owner release; none is claimed here.
