## Context

Independent review of MO-26-10-19-22.11.49 cleared the Google account-selector
compatibility fix and noted a nonblocking URL extraction limitation. A bare
evidence URL followed immediately by sentence punctuation can include that
punctuation in the parsed URL, causing the email selector to fail validation.
The actual Evo558 screenshots use Markdown image links and are unaffected.

## Approach

Evaluate Markdown-aware URL extraction or conservative terminal-punctuation
handling without changing legitimate object names, approved origin/path scope,
account-selector validation or signed-query rejection. Keep this separate from
the provider-specific compatibility fix.

## Acceptance

- A bare approved URL followed by ordinary sentence punctuation is handled intentionally.
- Markdown links, legitimate punctuation in object paths, and query-safety guards retain coverage.
