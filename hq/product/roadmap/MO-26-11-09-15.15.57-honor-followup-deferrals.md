## Context

The review validator rejects a new deferral ticket filed after a cleared follow-up even when the Git range permits it.

## Approach

Let the Git-aware validator recognize a new ticket named by the review, then keep the existing commit-level allowlist. Test that unrelated edits and existing-ticket changes remain refused.
