## Context

An audit of 389 merged independent-review records in Morpheus, Evo and Lakina found eight PRs with more than three recorded turns. Seven needed an authorized code-review turn; one used a fourth turn only for finalization. Late focused-test or CI discoveries, scope added after clearance, and missing review context recur. Evo #409's camera-count bug was found by a focused simulator test run after initial review, consuming an avoidable third code-review turn. The existing three-turn cap is a ceiling, not a target.

## Approach

Require authors to finish the relevant focused tests and provide the full review packet before starting independent review. Present one initial review and one focused response as the normal path; reserve the third for a genuine late correction or unresolved substantive finding. Clarify that routine review-record writing belongs to the author and that the finalization-only turn cannot repair an incomplete review. Keep the existing cap and human exception rules for genuinely complex or high-risk work.
