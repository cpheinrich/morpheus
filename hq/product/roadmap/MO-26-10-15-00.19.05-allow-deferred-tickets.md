## Context

The review contract requires a roadmap ticket for each deferred or open finding,
but the coverage validator rejects a ticket first created after a conditional
clearance. Evo PR #503 spent a reviewer follow-up turn to resolve an incidental
finding it otherwise could have tracked, exposing this contradiction.

## Approach

Allow only a new roadmap item named by a deferred or open finding
between `reviewed` and `covered` (or after a conditionally
cleared follow-up and before `covered`). Keep the ordinary coverage rule for
existing items, unrelated paths, and commits after `covered`.
