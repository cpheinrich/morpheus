## Context

Independent review of MO-26-11-04-00.19.43 (PR 607), turn 4, identified incidental finding I1:
GitHub's failed-job rerun can retain successful decision and preflight outputs. If night A
passed those jobs but failed tests or upload, and B subsequently delivered, retrying only the
failed jobs can upload A under a newer build number. These test/upload variants predate PR 607.
Its newly introduced preflight-rerun variant is addressed in that PR by keeping current-main
equality as the default and making pinned-source support an explicit caller opt-in.

This item tracks only the remaining pre-existing shared-nightly variants. It does not authorize
a live rollback, fault injection, forced unchanged build, or changes to delivery acceptance.

## Approach

Reproduce the exact failed-job output reuse with an isolated fixture. Put the smallest source-age
protection at the boundary that necessarily runs before a new upload, while preserving accepted
upload reconciliation and confirmation-only retries. Check existing caller upload boundaries
before choosing shared versus consumer placement. Implement in a separately claimed task with
focused tests, independent review, CI and protected merge.

## Acceptance

- A newly allocated upload cannot replace a newer delivered source with an older nightly source,
  including when earlier decision/preflight jobs are reused on a failed-job rerun.
- Confirmation of an already accepted exact build does not re-upload or allocate another build.
- Unknown provider state is retained as unknown; current-head ordinary delivery remains usable.
