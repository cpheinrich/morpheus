## Context

Every Morpheus task runs on whatever model and effort its session started with. The profiling
rounds ([worklog](../../../.agent/worklog/2026-10-28-profiling-round-2.md)) measure where time and tokens go, but cannot say whether a cheaper
model or lower effort would have produced the same merged result, or whether a stronger one would
have avoided the churn they counted — review-coverage failures in Evo up from 6 to 14 sampled,
post-review fixes, CI retries. That question decides how subscription quota is spent, and today it
is answered by habit.

## Approach

Run under the research-engineer skill (`morpheus cto guide research-engineer`): benchmark before
building, decide on paired evidence against measured noise, record per the research-records format.

- **Set:** 10–15 shipped Morpheus items with one merged PR, a clean parent commit, an independent
  review record and a deterministic test signal, mixed in size (small fix, CLI feature, workflow
  change). Exclude items whose acceptance needed live providers.
- **Arms:** model × effort (for example the current Opus, Fable and Sonnet at low, medium and high,
  plus Codex), each starting from the item file at its parent commit in an isolated worktree, with
  the same instructions and no access to the merged change.
- **Score:** the merged PR's own tests run against the candidate; substantive findings from an
  independent reviewer blind to the arm; CI; tokens, wall time and quota from transcripts.
  Similarity to the merged diff is a secondary signal only.
- **Noise:** repeat one arm at least twice to measure run-to-run variance before comparing arms.

## Needs from Chris

A quota ceiling and the models in scope before the first run: the grid is on the order of 15 items
× 9 arms plus repeats, all on real subscription quota.

## Acceptance

- A research record with per-arm pass rate, review findings, cost and variance, and a recommended
  default model and effort per task class, put to Chris as an inbox decision.
