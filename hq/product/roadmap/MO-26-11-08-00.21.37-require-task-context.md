## Context

Bounded startup certifies the short core, but an agent can claim work without seeing any context about the task.

## Approach

Cue a task lookup at the first prompt. Before a governed action, verify that the read hook saw a cited excerpt from an indexed task record. Preserve an exact-path route and the full-startup control.
