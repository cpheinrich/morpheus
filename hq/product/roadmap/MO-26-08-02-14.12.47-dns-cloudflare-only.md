## Context

§6 listed Cloudflare as "Registrar, CDN, R2, transactional email" and the prose asserted it was
"the registrar and DNS for every domain". Neither half held:

- **Registration was never Cloudflare-only, and cannot be.** No single registrar carries every TLD.
  `heinrich.la`, `heinrich.llc` and `heinrich.capital` are at Porkbun because Cloudflare does not
  sell those extensions; `darwin.health` is at Sav.com.
- **Conflating registration with DNS made DNS look like a per-project choice.** It is not, and the
  cost of leaving it ambiguous was real: `heinrich.la` was set up with DNS at Porkbun, and Porkbun's
  DNS editor does not list a zone's existing records, so a parking record could not be seen or
  removed from its UI. Twenty minutes went into diagnosing a zone that could not be inspected.

## Approach

Split the two decisions explicitly, in a new §6.1.

DNS becomes unconditional: Cloudflare, always, whoever the registrar is — stated so it can never be
raised as a question again. Registration becomes a short approved list, Cloudflare first where it
carries the TLD and Porkbun for the long tail, with anything else a deviation to be transferred out.

The section also carries the setup order, because two of the steps are non-obvious and both were hit
in practice: choose **manual** DNS import when connecting a zone, since automatic import copies the
registrar's parking records into the new zone where they compete with the real ones; and keep the apex
CNAME unproxied, which §10.2's reasoning already implied but only for one project.
