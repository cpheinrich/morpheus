## Context

Fleet retains the settled task and artifacts today. A controller needs the native session identity and guardian timing to record review evidence, plus the available heartbeat and quota status for an enrolled project.

## Approach

Carry verified invocation evidence from the guardian and executor through supervisor settlement. Return it only to the owning controller. Report project-scoped slot readiness from authenticated heartbeats and compatible quota probes. Keep old workers and services interoperable.

## Acceptance

- Distinct fresh sessions on one slot produce different native session IDs.
- Lost settlement replies and supervisor recovery preserve exact evidence without executing again.
- Readiness separates fresh candidates, stale observations, and incompatible gateways.
- The controller and worker privacy scopes remain enforced.
