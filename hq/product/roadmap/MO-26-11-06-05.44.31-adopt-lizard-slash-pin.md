## Context

The pinned Lizard (`541623c`, tag `morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-jsx-boolean`, adopted in #630)
reads a slash in JSX text (`<th>At 9% / 11%</th>`) as the start of a regex literal. Lizard's regex
pre-scan runs on raw source and is not JSX-aware, so the literal ran on through the slash of the
next closing tag. Before PR 560 that stray token was harmless; with JSX children read as text it
unbalances the elements and runs the enclosing function on past its end. This is the unidentified
form #630 disclosed: on Lakina it made `SaasBottomUp.tsx` `CompanyDetail` measure 99–184 at CCN 17
and dropped `SaasBottomUp`. It is a regression of upstream PR 560 itself (plain upstream master reads
the minimal case correctly).

Upstream PR 560 at `d2d52d9` fixes it for JSX only: in `.jsx`/`.tsx` a regex literal may not end at
an unescaped `<` that starts closing-tag syntax, so `/</g` stays a literal and the JavaScript and
TypeScript token patterns are unchanged. The fork integrates it as `b57041b` (tag
`morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-jsx-text-slash`, object `e5a9e4a`), a fast-forward of `541623c`.

## Approach

Move `LIZARD_PIN` to `b57041b` and its tag; wheels, Python floor and older tags unchanged. Add the
root's repro as `slash-text.tsx` and `slash-text.jsx` canaries with exact names, line bounds and CCN
(Rates 1–7 CCN 2, escape 8 CCN 2, after 9 CCN 2; `escape` keeps the real `/</g` regex). A test
installs `541623c` and asserts the canary refuses it; the `3b53c11`, `484bbd0` and `7f14ea5`
refusals and every earlier canary stay. No policy, mode, gate or enforcement change; consumers are
not changed to suit the parser. Known limits (brace-less element attribute values, a `key={...}`
ending an enclosing expression-bodied arrow early) are documented, not claimed fixed.

**Acceptance.** New pin installs and passes `complexity install --check`; `541623c` is refused by
the canary; earlier canaries measure identically on `541623c` and `b57041b`; the root's rescan of
Lakina's real `SaasBottomUp.tsx` and whole enforcing Evo scan at the new pin are recorded; full CI
and a fresh independent review before merge.
