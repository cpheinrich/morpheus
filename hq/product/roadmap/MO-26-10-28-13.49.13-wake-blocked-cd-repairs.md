## Context

Chris asked to fix every failure mode found in the Evo CD recovery audit and validate a new TestFlight build. The live staging repair reached 285 invocations while repeatedly returning the same unanswered missing capability. A timer/checkpoint is not delivery progress. PR536 fixes admission diagnostics and PR538 fixes ordinary queue fairness; neither bounds repeated unchanged blocked analysis.

## Approach

Keep this policy in the existing repair worker. After two strategies, a completed blocked invocation with a concrete missing capability may wait for changed frozen task/evidence/delivery context, with a six-hour maximum unchanged cooldown. Store only versioned context metadata in the existing opaque invocation-start transition receipt. Legacy receipts establish a baseline through one normal invocation. Preserve immutable tasks, admission, ownership and uncertain-effect controls; evidence may trigger analysis but never grants human review authorization.

## Acceptance

- Unchanged capability waits consume no quota/login/model or hosted Actions lookup and do not hide another eligible ordinary repair.
- New supported evidence or relevant delivery context wakes promptly even when a prior next-check clock is later; an evidence change during admission remains visible after completion.
- Exact cooldown equality allows an eventual recheck. Legacy/malformed metadata cannot silently park work, and task tampering still refuses.
- Focused tests exercise local and hosted paths, boundaries, concurrency and selected assertion-killed mutants. Independent review and meaningful required CI precede protected merge.
- Source completion is recorded separately from the Evo binding, actual worker adoption, observed recovery and installable TestFlight delivery, which Root continues under the campaign mandate.
