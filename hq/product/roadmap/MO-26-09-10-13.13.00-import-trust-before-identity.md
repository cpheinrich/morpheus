## Context

The Mac mini now passes the explicit certificate, private-key, and trust checks
and completes its unsigned archive, but Xcode export still cannot discover the
profile-authorized identity. WWDR G3 was imported only after the leaf/private
key pair, so Security.framework first created the identity with an incomplete
chain.

## Approach

Import Xcode's WWDR G3 intermediate into the isolated keychain before importing
the distribution PKCS#12. Preserve the direct release-contract checks and pin
the exporter to the profile-authorized fingerprint. Validate by rerunning the
failed Evo upload job through archive, export, App Store Connect processing,
and TestFlight group assignment.
