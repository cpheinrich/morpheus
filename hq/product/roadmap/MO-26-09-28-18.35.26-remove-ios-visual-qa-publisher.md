## Context

Chris asked for the automated "nightly screenshot PR" to go, in every project
that has one. The reusable `ios-visual-qa` workflow and its
`.github/scripts/ios-visual-qa.cjs` publisher replaced a standing draft
`nightly-ios-visual-qa` PR with a gallery of each nightly run's screenshots.
Evo (#194) and Kairos (#61) each carried one permanently in their open-PR list.

## Approach

Remove the reusable workflow, the publisher script and its tests, and rewrite
the architecture §18 contract so it no longer promises a gallery. The nightly
keeps exporting screenshots into each run's `ios-screenshots-<run>-<attempt>` artifact, and
`capture-every-night` keeps its behaviour, running tests even on nights without
a release; only its description changes. The no-op marker artifact stays as a
visible record of an intentional skip.

This lands after the Evo and Kairos PRs that remove their callers, so no caller
is left pointing at a deleted workflow.
