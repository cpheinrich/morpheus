## Context

The canonical meeting-note format says how to distil and redact a meeting, but not how to deliver
the result. A Lakina session record was nearly bundled with roadmap changes, which would have mixed
the factual record with later interpretation in one review surface.

## Approach

State the boundary at the system, contributor, canonical-format, and scaffold levels: a meeting note
lands in an isolated pull request containing only the factual record. Roadmap changes, strategy
refinement, implementation, decision promotion, and other follow-up interpretation land separately.
Pin the downstream scaffold wording with a focused test.

Do not change Lakina as part of this item.
