## Context

The pinned Lizard (`484bbd0`, tag `morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-ts`) abandons a JSX tag at a spread
attribute (`{...x}`) or a hyphenated attribute name (`data-state`, `aria-hidden`). Since PR 560
reads JSX children as text, an abandoned tag inside children runs the enclosing function on past its
end and drops the functions after it. Fixed in upstream PR 560 at `bb9efad`, integrated as
`3b53c11` under the immutable tag `morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-tsx`, first parent `484bbd0`.

## Approach

Move `LIZARD_PIN` to `3b53c11` and its tag (wheels and Python floor unchanged). Add the root's
`attributes.tsx` repro to the canary as both `.tsx` and `.jsx`, asserting exact names, line bounds
and CCN (Row 1–3 CCN 1, Grid 4–11 CCN 3, Card 12–14 CCN 2, after 15 CCN 2); canary rows may now
carry line bounds. Add a test that installs `484bbd0` and asserts the canary refuses it. Existing
JavaScript, Swift, TSX and TS canaries stay. No policy, mode or enforcement change.

**Acceptance.** New pin installs and passes `complexity install --check`; `484bbd0` and `7f14ea5`
are refused by the canary; existing canaries measure identically on both pins; `complexity check
--all` on frozen Evo `ab95f3d` compared against `484bbd0` with every delta reported; independent
review and CI before merge.
