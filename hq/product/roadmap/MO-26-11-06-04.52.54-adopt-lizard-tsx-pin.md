## Context

The pinned Lizard (`484bbd0`, tag `morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-ts`) abandons a JSX tag at a spread
attribute (`{...x}`), a hyphenated attribute name (`data-state`, `aria-hidden`) or a valueless
attribute after a braced one (`margin={{ top: 6 }} accessibilityLayer`). Since PR 560 reads JSX
children as text, an abandoned tag inside children runs the enclosing function on past its end and
drops the functions after it. On Lakina this is what made `PortfolioValueChart` run on past its
end. The forms are fixed in upstream PR 560, first at `bb9efad` and then at `24c6ff3`. The fork
integrated them as `3b53c11` (tag `morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-tsx`) and finally as `541623c` (tag
`morpheus-analyzer-2026-11-06-jsx-boolean`). Each is a fast-forward of the one before.

*Corrected after review.* An earlier version said this fixed a Lakina component reported at CCN 17
whose true value is 14. That was wrong. That row is `CompanyDetail` in `SaasBottomUp.tsx`, and it
still overruns at `541623c` (99–184, CCN 17, with `SaasBottomUp` dropped) through a JSX form that
is not yet identified. It is recorded as a known limit, not as a fix.

## Approach

Move `LIZARD_PIN` to `541623c` and its tag (wheels and Python floor unchanged). Add the root's
repros to the canary as both `.tsx` and `.jsx`, asserting exact names, line bounds and CCN:
`attributes` (Row 1–3 CCN 1, Grid 4–11 CCN 3, Card 12–14 CCN 2, after 15 CCN 2) and `valueless`
(Chart 1–9 CCN 2, after 10 CCN 2); canary rows may now carry line bounds. Tests install `3b53c11`,
`484bbd0` and `7f14ea5` and assert the canary refuses each. Existing JavaScript, Swift, TSX and TS
canaries stay. No policy, mode or enforcement change; consumers are not changed to suit the parser.

**Acceptance.** New pin installs and passes `complexity install --check`; `3b53c11`, `484bbd0` and
`7f14ea5` are refused by the canary; existing canaries measure identically on `3b53c11` and
`541623c`; consumer rescans with the final pin reported; independent review and CI before merge.
