## Context

The actual MusicGuide placement snapshot exceeds the existing 2 MiB bound, despite small synthetic fixtures passing. Its semantic HTML survives, but repeated computed styles inflate the frozen viewport and the image error disappears behind later Saved status.

## Approach

Measure the actual guide, keep resolved style differences against a browser initial-value baseline, and retain one inert shared reset rather than repeated custom properties/default declarations. Preserve existing time/node/byte/JPEG caps and immutable placement. Keep failure text persistently beside the editor thumbnail. Verify realistic inherited-token CSS and actual guide page/modal captures in Chromium and WebKit before independent review.
