## Context

Morpheus invested in a searchable access layer for its Git-backed project records.
Recent work measured search and setup, but evidence for accepted task time and
operator leverage is incomplete.

## Approach

Review public release evidence and privacy-filtered observations across projects,
providers, subagents and Factory workers. Compare with a separate Fable 5.2 Factory
audit and publish the [findings and ranked proposals](../../../docs/morpheus-context-audit.md).
A [companion draft](https://github.com/cpheinrich/morpheus/pull/715) covers
the operator scorecard and prospective measurement pilot.
