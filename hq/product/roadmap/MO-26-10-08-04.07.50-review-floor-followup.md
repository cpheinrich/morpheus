## Context

The 30-second initial-review floor at normal/high risk fires on the initial turn regardless of
what follows. An initial review recorded `incomplete` solely for being under the floor can never be
rescued by the authorized same-reviewer turn the contract offers for incomplete turns. Three tiny
workflow PRs (darwin-health/evo#412, cpheinrich/lakinacapital#509, cpheinrich/kairos#70) had clean
21–25 s initial reviews, recorded incomplete, and Chris authorized one more turn each; that path is
dead today.

## Approach

Record the initial turn's own verdict as `initialOutcome: "incomplete"` (the record's `outcome`
stays the final verdict). An under-floor initial turn is accepted only when it is recorded that way
and a same-reviewer follow-up carrying `humanAuthorization` measures at least 30 seconds and clears.
An incomplete initial turn's next turn always requires `humanAuthorization`, matching the existing
rule for incomplete follow-ups. Still refused: an under-floor initial turn not recorded incomplete,
an under-floor or unauthorized rescue turn, and a different reviewer.
