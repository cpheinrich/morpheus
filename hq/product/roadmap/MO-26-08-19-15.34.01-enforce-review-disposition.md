## Context

Rung 2 reviews a pull request and nothing makes anyone look: Morpheus's `main` requires **zero
status checks** (a PR can merge with CI red or mid-run), Evo requires `web` and `pm` but not the
review, and neither repo requires conversation resolution. So a PR can merge while the review is
still running, and a delivered review can be merged past unread. Chris's call, 2026-08-19: enforce
that reviews are *acted on* — the developing agent need not answer every comment, but it must read
the findings and disposition them before merging.

The settled decision that rung 2 does not block stands untouched. What it forbids is the review's
**content** gating the merge — a model-graded gate failing on its own noise. What this adds gates
the **process**: the review must have finished, and each finding must have been visibly closed by
someone. The reviewer still cannot stop a merge by being wrong; it can only insist on being read.

## Approach

Three mechanisms, one waiver:

- **Require conversation resolution** (branch protection, both repos). Findings land inline now,
  so each is a resolvable thread; the merge refuses while any is open. Resolving is the read
  receipt — reply where declining, resolve everywhere.
- **`agent-review / delivery` becomes a required check**, and fails rather than warning when a
  requested, configured review was not delivered. Because delivery `needs: review`, an in-progress
  review holds the check pending, which is what stops both `--auto` and a manual merge mid-review.
  Legitimate skips (records-only PRs, unconfigured repos, `synchronize` pushes) leave the job
  skipped, which satisfies a required check — nothing new blocks work the rung never covered.
- **Rung 1 becomes required too** (`node / check`, `pm / pm`, `pr / conventions` here; `web`,
  `pm` on Evo). Zero required checks is how `--auto` once merged a stale head — a recorded bite.

**The waiver:** `review-waived: <reason>` in the PR body lets delivery pass when the reviewer
itself is broken — same shape and same validation as `skip-tests:`, reported as waived rather than
swallowed. The credit outage would otherwise have blocked every merge for six days; with the
waiver it blocks until someone says out loud that they are merging unreviewed, which is the point.

## Acceptance

- A PR whose review is still running cannot merge, by `--auto` or by hand.
- A delivered review with an unresolved inline finding blocks the merge until the thread is
  resolved; resolving it unblocks with no re-review.
- A records-only PR merges with delivery skipped.
- `review-waived: reviewer is down` passes delivery and is reported as waived;
  `review-waived: yes` is refused.
