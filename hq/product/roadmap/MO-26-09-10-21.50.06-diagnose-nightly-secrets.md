## Context

The built-in nightly upload previously installed tools and started release setup before reporting
missing signing credentials. Cross-repository callers cannot supply caller environment secrets
to that job and need a diagnostic pointing to the caller-owned protected upload boundary.

## Approach

Check required credential presence in the first upload step using boolean flags only. Report
missing names and the `run-upload: false` / caller-owned action path. Preserve optional secrets
and blank P12 passwords. Exercise the extracted workflow shell for every missing required value.
