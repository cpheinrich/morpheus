## Context

The hosted security-remediation workflow has a canonical policy and GitHub App, but its
identity asset is not versioned and the operator steps are dispersed across architecture and the
execution contract. The bot should be recognizable on pull requests, and a repository owner should
be able to adopt and verify the policy from one durable page.

## Approach

Store the approved badge with the documentation, make the existing execution contract discoverable,
and add the exact App permissions plus an adoption and clean-run checklist without creating a
second copy of the policy. Record private operations-repository custody, reviewed repository
admission, and GitHub's account boundary so a cross-organization rollout does not fail mysteriously.
