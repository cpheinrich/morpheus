## Context

The [verification audit](https://github.com/cpheinrich/morpheus/pull/735) reproduced a candidate
policy waiver in a disposable repository. The existing conventions lane reads the review policy
from the PR head and Morpheus's own callers can select the candidate checker.

## Acceptance

- Review requirements and Manager protected paths come from the target branch.
- Review evidence still binds to the exact PR head. The CI checker is isolated from candidate
  files, and Morpheus tests proposed checker changes in its candidate Node checks.
- Proposed waivers, established opt-outs, invalid or unavailable inputs, and stale evidence
  have regression coverage. Existing shared caller names and events remain compatible.
- Read back adoption on two consumers through fresh shared conventions runs.
