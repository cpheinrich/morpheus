## Context

Evo's first Mac mini upload after the provisioning-profile decode fix imported
one PKCS#12 identity, but `security find-identity -p codesigning` reported no
valid identity and stopped before archive. GitHub-hosted Xcode runners accept
the same certificate because Xcode has installed Apple's signing intermediate
into the logged-in user's keychain; the headless runner has no login keychain.

## Approach

Import the Apple WWDR G3 intermediate bundled with the selected Xcode into the
ephemeral release keychain before evaluating the distribution identity. Keep
the existing exact certificate/profile SHA match, add an ordering regression,
and prove acceptance with a self-hosted Evo upload that reaches TestFlight.
