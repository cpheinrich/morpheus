## Context

Evo's Mac mini release still imports one distribution identity after the WWDR
G3 fix, but macOS reports zero identities valid for code signing. The current
failure message does not distinguish certificate expiry, issuer mismatch,
missing private-key pairing, or trust-chain rejection.

## Approach

When the code-signing policy rejects the imported identity, report only public
certificate metadata, the identities present in the isolated keychain, and the
verbose trust evaluation. Do not print certificate bodies, private keys,
passwords, or release secrets. Use the resulting self-hosted runner evidence to
make the next signing fix, then retain the diagnostics for future rotations.
