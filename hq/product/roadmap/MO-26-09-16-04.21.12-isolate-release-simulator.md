## Context

Evo's release gate failed three UI tests with `Test crashed with signal term` while a separate
main CI job ran on the same Mac mini (#249). Both lanes checked out the same commit and selected
the same base device under the shared `github-runner` CoreSimulator device set; the release ran
non-parallel on the base device, CI ran two clones. The simulator's unified log shows `launchd_sim`
terminating processes during system shutdown, not an app crash. The release correctly blocked the
upload; rerunning after the CI lanes went idle passed and shipped Evo 1.0.1 (18).

This is distinct from #240 (stale result bundle after cleanup, fixed by #246): here the tests ran
and the simulator was shut down underneath them.

## Approach

Establish who shuts the base device down: inspect clone preparation and simulator ownership in
`ios-ci.yml` between two self-hosted lanes sharing one device set. Then either give every job its
own cloned device (never test on the shared base device, even in the non-parallel release
configuration) or take a device-ownership lock that a lane holds from clone through shutdown.
Prefer per-job isolation: it removes the shared resource rather than serialising access to it.
Verify with a deliberate overlap on the Mac mini before closing; the logs, not a green run, are the
evidence.
