## Context

All three retrofits failed `pm / pm` in CI. `init` wrote product READMEs with bare
`<!-- morpheus:begin --><!-- morpheus:end -->` markers, but the generator writes
`_Nothing here yet._` between them even for an empty artifact. So a project nobody had touched was
already stale, and `pm index --check` failed on the first push.

## Shipped

`init` generates the indexes as part of scaffolding, skipping artifacts a `kind` does not have —
`internal` projects have a roadmap and nothing else.

## Third time

MO-008 shipped a scaffold whose inbox failed `inbox validate`. MO-032 shipped one whose `node-ci`
job failed on a non-Node repo. Now this.

The test I added for MO-008 asserted that scaffolded *files* satisfy the validators. It did not
assert that the scaffolded *repository* passes the checks its own CI runs, which is the claim that
actually matters. There is now a test for that.
