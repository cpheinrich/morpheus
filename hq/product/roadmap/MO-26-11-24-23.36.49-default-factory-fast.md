## Context

Factory worker turns currently use Standard when speed is omitted. The operator requested Fast for Codex subscription workers on all Factory devices and applicable project routes, with fresh-session verification. Claude remains at Standard because its Fast mode uses paid credits.

## Approach

Use an operator-controlled Codex worker speed default for local and Fleet placement. Carry the selected speed from admission through launch and verification, retain an explicit Standard override, and qualify the enrolled Codex subscription routes before enabling the preference. Keep live rollout and billing evidence in private operator records.
