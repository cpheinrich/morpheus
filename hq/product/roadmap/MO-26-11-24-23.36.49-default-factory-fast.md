## Context

Factory worker turns currently use Standard when speed is omitted. The operator requested Fast as the default on all Factory devices and applicable project routes, with fresh-session verification.

## Approach

Use operator-controlled worker speed defaults for local and Fleet placement. Carry the selected speed from admission through launch and verification, retain an explicit Standard override, and qualify the supported provider and gateway routes before enabling the preference. Keep live rollout and billing evidence in private operator records.
