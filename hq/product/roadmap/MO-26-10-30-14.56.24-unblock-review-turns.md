## Context

Evo's CD staging channel stalled from 2026-10-26: its test-only repair PR (darwin-health/evo#665)
had an `incomplete` initial review, the next turn needed `humanAuthorization` and the same reviewer
session, and once a human authorized it the session could not be recovered. Chris (owner),
2026-10-30: reviews must never block on approval, any fresh session may continue, and the automatic
limit is five turns for every Morpheus project, with feedback that blocks only on substantive
problems.

## Approach

- Five ordinary review turns (initial included, incomplete included) validate without
  `humanAuthorization`; each ordinary turn after the fifth needs it. Finalization stays outside.
- No same-reviewer rule. A turn by a different session records `handoff { reason, findings }`
  naming every earlier finding; a handed-over `blocked` turn names its substantive findings in
  `blockedOn`, so nothing an earlier reviewer found can be dropped silently.
- Budgets bind turns that render a verdict; a timed-out turn is recorded incomplete with its real
  duration and the next turn continues.
- Reviewer contract, legacy persona, runbook, architecture, AGENTS.md, scaffold and `doctor` say
  the same; reviewers block only on substantive problems and notes may be declined in one line.
- Existing records, including ones carrying `humanAuthorization` and the legacy `verification`
  turn, still validate.
