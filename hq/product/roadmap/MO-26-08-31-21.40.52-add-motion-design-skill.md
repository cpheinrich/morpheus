## Context

The motion-graphic exploration used for Evo produced useful, comparable mockups, but the process
lived only in a long conversational prompt. A repository-owned skill makes that method reusable by
collaborators, while initializer scaffolding makes it discoverable in every new Morpheus project.

## Approach

Add one repository-owned Codex `motion-design-exploration` skill under `.agents/skills/`. It will
preserve the successful constraints: inspect the real shell and brand, distinguish reference
material from instructions, research current motion ideas, produce six genuinely different
directions in one theme, prefer an interactive review artifact, and stop before production
implementation. Embed the same content in `morpheus init`, cover company and internal projects plus
source-copy parity in tests, and document the new repository instruction layer.
