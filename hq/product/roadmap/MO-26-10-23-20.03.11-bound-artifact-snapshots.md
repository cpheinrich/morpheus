## Context

Growing native evidence, acceptance receipts and failed browser traces previously
reached artifact uploaders without a positive stored-byte envelope. Cap admission before
upload without truncating evidence or changing delivery/runtime policy.

## Approach

Ship one canonical six-channel immutable stager, an independent 64 MiB/one-day CI trace
role, successful-own-stage guards and a native all-selected barrier. Pin the normal
processing environment to 1200/30. Preserve original names/ZIP layouts and existing
native/runtime/controller pins. Add focused boundary/race/mutant tests and a bounded
Node 24/Python 3.9/3.14 artifact transport CI job.

## Acceptance

- Every integrated uploader consumes only its own admitted private snapshot; every
  selected native stage must succeed before any native upload.
- Exact byte boundaries, links/races, missing/empty evidence, spent leaves and source
  integrity are tested without provider calls; consumer qualification remains unchanged.
- Actual Node 24 Actions upload plus normal download byte readback passes in source CI.
- Documentation states provider retention, same-UID, blocking I/O and wall-clock limits.
- Separate consumer adoption and live delivery remain outside this Morpheus task.

## Phase A handoff

Local source/test commit only. Initial source push, draft PR/CI and fresh independent
review require the accountable lead's next release. No live consumer/controller/provider
operation is authorized by this item or by a local fixture pass.
