## Context

MO-26-10-04-16.24.17 (#318) added `-test-repetition-relaunch-enabled YES` to the retry flags so
a flake rooted in the test-host process could get a fresh process. On Evo's release lane the
first run with an actual failure under that flag (run 37394836077, 2026-10-06) showed xcodebuild
re-executing the entire UI test bundle after the first pass instead of only the failed cases:
the suite's first pass ended at 01:52 UTC, a full second pass was still running at the job's
three-hour limit, the job was cancelled, and no test evidence was exported. A release that
fails can therefore never produce the evidence the recovery controller needs.

## Approach

Remove the flag and pin its absence in the workflow test. Retries stay in-process; a flake that
lives in the process is left to the next release.
