## Context

The iOS overlay used viewport coordinates for every pin and captured only the frame at Send.
After scrolling, a marker stayed fixed over different content, and the Send-time screenshot
could not show where an earlier comment was made. A comment also lacked a screen identifier.

## Approach

Capture a separate immutable frame and screen ID when each iOS pin is placed. Follow the
nearby native accessibility element's position as the simulator scrolls, hiding markers when
their element leaves the visible screen. Keep original coordinates for the per-pin frame and
preserve the batch-level frame for older agents. Teach all agents to read per-comment evidence.
