## Context

Chris requires the DevOps agent to prove that it tried every practical authorized way to unblock delivery before reporting itself blocked to the CTO. The CTO must independently check that proof. Existing skills require a blocker record in prose, while the CD worker accepts a bare blocked result and missing-capability string; that mismatch permits unsupported stalls.

## Approach

Use a bounded versioned proof envelope in existing result evidence, exact invocation/task/context binding, and the current worker's completion owner. Missing, malformed, borrowed or stale proof is retained as an attempted claim and treated as a diagnostic checkpoint requiring continued work. A valid envelope is a candidate requiring independent CTO corroboration, not a self-approved global hard block. Update canonical CTO/DevOps guidance and existing generated mirrors. Integrate protected PR540 normally before worker changes; this follow-up must not withhold that available recovery increment or ordinary TestFlight delivery.

## Acceptance

- Blocker candidates identify the symptom, exact observed target, newest authority, practical alternatives, actual attempts/outcomes/evidence, excluded unsafe/unauthorized/unavailable routes and reasons, narrow missing capability/minimum external step, independent work and next useful wake.
- A documented empty practical set requires concrete capability/authority evidence; no prohibited action is attempted just to populate an attempts list. Shape validation never claims to prove semantic exhaustion or truth.
- The worker rejects unsupported/stale claims without erasing the original report or losing new evidence; valid candidates remain explicitly CTO-verification-required.
- The CTO independently corroborates critical evidence and available alternatives before recording a narrow accepted block or rejecting it with a next action. Model self-attestation cannot satisfy that check.
- The mandate records internal reporting cadence and next due time; this delivery uses existing15-minute fresh supervision and30-minute no-material-progress diagnosis. Quiet user notifications do not suspend internal supervision.
- Provider-free boundary/race/compatibility tests and selected assertion-killed mutants pass; canonical skill distribution stays aligned, independent review clears and meaningful CI passes before protected merge.
