## Context

A draft can pass CI and carry independent review without its author ever moving it to ready. The PR template explains when to start a draft. Authors need the corresponding transition.

## Approach

Align Morpheus's instructions, project scaffold, PR template and review and merge runbooks. A completed draft moves to ready, then the author follows required CI and authorized merge. A substantive blocker or explicit hold stays visible under Open questions.

## Acceptance

- Instructions and scaffold name `gh pr ready <n>` after implementation, evidence and review are complete.
- Required CI and merge authorization remain in force.
- Real blockers and expressly requested holds have a documented way to clear them.
