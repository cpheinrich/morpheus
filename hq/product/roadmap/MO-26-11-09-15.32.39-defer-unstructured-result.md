## Context

A resumed Claude worker can remain active after an unstructured result event, while Factory incorrectly reports needs_input without a question.

## Approach

Keep an unstructured fallback private while the process runs. Report failure after exit if no structured result superseded it. Exercise the live status and both terminal outcomes through the Manager.
