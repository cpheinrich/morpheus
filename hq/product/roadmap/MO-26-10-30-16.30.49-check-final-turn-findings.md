## Context

Found as incidental R6 in the independent review of MO-26-10-30-14.56.24 (PR #568). A substantive
finding can be recorded as raised on the final, clearing review turn and the record still
validates, although no later turn checked its fix. This predates PR #568; the per-finding `turn`
field it added makes the case detectable.

## Approach

In `validateReviewRecord`, refuse a substantive finding whose `turn` is the last turn unless it is
cleared under a reviewer condition with `conditionMet`. Add a focused regression test at that
boundary.
