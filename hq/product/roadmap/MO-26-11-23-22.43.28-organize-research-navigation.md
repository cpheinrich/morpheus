## Context

A flat application graph becomes hard to read as the screen inventory grows. The existing study viewer should support task-specific navigation with progressive disclosure.

## Approach

Reuse React Flow and Dagre. Add optional validated study flows, a horizontal flow picker, one initial node per flow, immediate outgoing choices, and graph-local back and Escape. Keep node selection synchronized with the screen detail. Preserve legacy studies and visually distinguish observed paths from release-source documentation.
