## Context

On 2026-10-18 Chris gave a Codex session CTO-level authority and a task budget to rebuild Evo's
iOS continuous delivery, launch agents as needed, and then write a reusable "agentic CTO" skill.
`docs/cd-pipeline-v2.md` records the contract that skill must carry: a brief specification,
concrete success criteria and a budget; requirements-first design, independent review,
empirical prototypes, delegated implementation, outcome-based validation, durable ownership and
honest closure. On 2026-10-21 he asked for it now, in parallel with the delivery campaign, merged
into Morpheus so other projects can use it, and consolidated with the guidance he has given agents
across Morpheus, Evo and Lakina about autonomy, initiative, upfront design and budget authority.

## Approach

A repository skill whose canonical files live in `.agents/skills/agentic-cto/` with a
`.claude/skills/` mirror, printed by `morpheus cto guide` from the installed CLI. As with
`comment-qa`, `morpheus init` gives every project kind a one-file pointer, so revisions reach
projects through the CLI instead of drifting in copies; a parity test holds the copies together. `SKILL.md` stays short;
`references/mandate.md` holds the parameterized working agreement and budget ledger, and
`references/provenance.md` traces each rule to its source. The campaign's own cap, waivers and
quota routing are instance settings and stay out of the universal rules. Behaviour is checked by
scenario-based forward evaluation with fresh agents, recorded in the acceptance file.
