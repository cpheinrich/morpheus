Directed by Chris on 2026-08-01, while moving `cpheinrich.com` from Cloudflare Pages to Vercel.

`cpheinrich.com`'s contact form sends through Cloudflare's Email Sending REST API. The migration
raised the question of whether to move it to Resend, and the answer is no, for a reason that
generalises past this one form:

**Cloudflare is already load-bearing and is not going away.** It is the registrar and the DNS for
every domain — including `darwin.health` and `evo.med`, which host on Vercel — and it holds R2 for
public media. Email Sending is a service inside a vendor that is already in the stack. Resend
would be a net-new dependency, a second account to hold, and another credential to rotate, to
replace something that works.

The row `Email, accounts | Google Workspace` in §6 is about *human mailboxes* and says nothing
about application email, which is how a project ended up picking a provider per-project rather
than reading one off the spec.

## What changes

- `architecture.md` §6 gains a **Transactional email → Cloudflare Email Sending** row, and the
  Cloudflare row names email among what it covers
- `.agent/decisions.md` records the decision and the reasoning, so the next project does not
  re-derive it

## Not in scope

Nothing migrates. `cpheinrich.com` already uses it; no other project sends mail yet. This writes
down the default so the next one does not have to choose.
