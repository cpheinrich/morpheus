## Context

A held supplemental validation intent must not start new repair-agent work, but its delivery, fault and receipt paths must keep running. Today the only pause scopes (intent, channel, `all`) stop delivery too, and a rejected upload can queue repair that the worker would pick up indefinitely.

## Approach

A namespaced `repair:<intent>` pause, which the existing delivery gates ignore. A pure leaf (`src/cd/repair-control.ts`; type-only imports, hash and store injected) provides CAS hold/release with the full request digest and readback. Release is receipt-only. A closed operator grammar (`repair-control-entry.ts`) and a worker-only entry (`worker-only-entry.ts`) wrap an injected, unchanged `runWorker` and guard only the three admission transactions. See `docs/runbooks/cd-repair-control.md`.
