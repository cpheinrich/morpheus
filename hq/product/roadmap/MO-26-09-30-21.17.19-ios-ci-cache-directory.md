## Context

The Mac mini is moving every iOS job into ephemeral Tart VMs (agent-host-setup `ci-vm/`):
a fresh clone of one golden image per job, deleted afterwards. A fresh VM has no DerivedData
and no SwiftPM checkouts, so every job would pay the full cold build. The dispatcher attaches
a persistent cache disk per repository and slot at a fixed guest path, but the reusable iOS
workflows and the TestFlight upload script hard-code `$RUNNER_TEMP` for those directories,
which the runner recreates per job.

## Approach

Honor `MORPHEUS_IOS_CI_CACHE` when the runner sets it (via the runner's `.env`) and the
directory exists: `ios-ci.yml` roots DerivedData and SourcePackages there and skips the
`actions/cache` SwiftPM step, the nightly workflow and `upload-testflight.sh` root the
release checkouts and archive DerivedData there. Everything else, including results and
evidence, stays under the job's temp directory. Runners that do not set it see no change.
