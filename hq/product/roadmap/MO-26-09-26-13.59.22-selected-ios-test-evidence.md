## Context

Evo needs a smaller selected PR suite without losing full nightly verification or first-attempt failure evidence. Successful runs currently discard test logs and structured results.

## Approach

Extend the reusable native workflow with optional only-testing, a retained selection manifest, and a caller-owned result validator. Export structured results and logs on success and failure; forward validation into the full nightly gate. Preserve default selection and secret-free execution.

## Acceptance

- Empty selection preserves existing callers. Explicit identifiers arrive as separate literal xcodebuild arguments.
- Caller evidence validation failures block the job, including after nominal XCTest success.
- Successful and failing runs upload commit-bound structured evidence and logs.
- Nightly forwards validation without narrowing its full test suite.
- Executable workflow tests cover selection quoting and evidence/validator success and failure.
