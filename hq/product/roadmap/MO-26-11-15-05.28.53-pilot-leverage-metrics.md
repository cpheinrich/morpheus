## Context

Morpheus can observe some search and worker timings today. It cannot yet link those
timings consistently to accepted outcomes or operator effort across projects.

## Approach

Propose starter KPIs, a small event and join contract, and a two-week opt-in pilot.
The deliverable is the [reviewable design](../../../docs/morpheus-leverage-pilot.md),
with a plan for measuring coverage and taking a decision at the end of the pilot.
Any instrumentation or enrollment needs its own implementation follow-up.
