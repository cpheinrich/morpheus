## Context

During Evo comment QA on 2026-10-04 Chris reported the Developer Mode switch flipping on
and straight back off, flipping late, and once flipping the Dataset Collector switch with it.
The app code was not at fault: driven through serve-sim's HID channel with ordered,
20 ms-plus taps the switch flipped every time. The overlay page sends each pointer event as
its own fetch, so a begin and its end can reach the server almost together (serve-sim drops
the tap) or reversed (the finger stays down until the next tap, which then lands on both
controls). Instant clicks from Claude's Browser pane and trackpad tap-to-click reproduce it.

## Approach

A `TouchPacer` in front of the HID socket keeps one finger's state: an end goes out no
sooner than 40 ms after its begin, an end with no finger down waits up to 120 ms for a late
begin and pairs with it or is dropped, a begin while a finger is down ends that finger
first, and a stray move is ignored. The page also chains its touch fetches so they leave in
order. Unit-tested with an injected clock; verified live against Evo's preview._
