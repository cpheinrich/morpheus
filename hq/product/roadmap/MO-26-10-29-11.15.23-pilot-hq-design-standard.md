## Context

The shared standard needed a real integrated pilot before a broad corpus migration. This item now
owns the reusable standard, personas and Darwin/Evo pilot. Project-specific adoption is tracked in
each project so this shared change can ship without pretending every HQ migration is complete.

## Approach

Deliver the shared HQ design and brevity contract, then prove it on Darwin's canonical Evo HQ and
long-form operating records. Preserve source meaning and stable URLs, capture final builds at
desktop/tablet/mobile, and use the evidence to revise the standard. Darwin's implementation is
tracked in `darwin-health/darwin#138` under `DW-26-10-29-12.11.36`. Lakina's dense-data and corpus
adoption is a separate project-owned item, `LK-26-11-05-01.39.21`; it is not hidden remaining scope
for this Morpheus item.

Darwin's expanded pilot showed that hierarchy and component reuse are not sufficient when the
prose itself remains expensive to scan. The standard now adds a measurable brevity contract:
current direction is a verb-led outcome of at most ten words; an executive brief is at most 75
words or five one-sentence bullets; and bloated human-facing legacy pages should usually lose
40–60% of their prose while retaining decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, thresholds, evidence,
caveats and compliance constraints. Legal/compliance documents and agent-centered records are
explicitly exempt from that compression target. Both independent-review personas enforce the same
contract, with ordinary copy preferences remaining non-blocking notes.
