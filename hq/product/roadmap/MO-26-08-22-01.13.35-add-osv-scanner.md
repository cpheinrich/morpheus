## Context

Morpheus distributes workflows and templates across projects, so a vulnerable dependency can
propagate beyond the repository where it was first introduced. Every project needs a low-overhead,
credential-free way to detect known dependency vulnerabilities after new disclosures.

## Approach

Add a pinned, reusable OSV full-scan workflow with the minimum permissions required to upload SARIF
findings. Exercise it in Morpheus weekly, on changes to `main`, and on demand. Do not use the
upstream PR-diff workflow while its symlink-bypass report remains open.
