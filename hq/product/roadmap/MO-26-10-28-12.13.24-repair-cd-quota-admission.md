## Context

Evo's ordinary delivery recovery stalled after native test failures. A fresh
2026-10-28 observation found production repair3 with zero invocations and a
60-second Codex quota initialization timeout. The worker called that condition
`wait-measured-subscription-capacity`, although no quota measurement completed.
An exact later staging invocation exited2 before creating a native session; its
policy shim emitted only `pinned native CLI could not start`.

Chris authorized current-state supervision, supported recovery writes and fixes
inside the existing USD350 campaign cap. This item owns the reusable admission
diagnostic source boundary; it grants no quota, review, CI or upload bypass.

## Approach

Preserve fresh measured subscription admission, current thresholds, ownership
fences and foreground-reader cleanup. Distinguish telemetry acquisition failure,
measured capacity refusal and authentication failure. Expose bounded sanitized
configured-reader startup/exit diagnostics without retaining raw stderr, config
or credential data. Prove the failure cases with provider-free fixtures, then
complete independent review, CI and protected merge. Consumer/runtime adoption
and an actual recovered TestFlight receipt remain separate acceptance.

Root owns PM/public/operational writes. One local source author owns the quota
reader, worker admission boundary, focused tests and relevant controller docs in
this task worktree. No provider, account or host probes are delegated.

The audit's fair-selection and repeated-capability-wait findings remain named
followups; avoid expanding this repair into a new scheduling or state framework.

## Acceptance

- Initialization/launch/read failures never claim measured quota exhaustion.
- Missing reader and early exit produce fixed bounded diagnostics, fail closed
  and preserve the existing private process-group cleanup guarantees.
- Authentication refusal stays distinct from unavailable telemetry and a real
  measured threshold refusal.
- Fresh valid readings retain admission and metering behavior; no stale fallback,
  prepaid-credit admission, review waiver, unknown upload retry or fence bypass.
- Focused tests exercise actual process cleanup as well as the TypeScript
  protocol boundary; source merge is not reported as live delivery.
