## Context

The four-hour fixed stale-run check trips on Evo's legitimate 180-minute test plus
180-minute upload pipeline, and on a recovery release queued behind a scheduled one (#312).

## Approach

Add a validated app-owned run timeout, age current attempts from their start timestamp,
and treat queued/pending/requested runs behind an active predecessor as waiting. Preserve
stuck-run detection, all dispatch/reservation guards and uncertain-upload refusal. Test exact
boundaries, old retries, queued pairs, missing timestamps and invalid configuration. Evo's
consumer adoption remains tracked in EV-26-10-01-13.30.14; no host activation is part of this fix.
