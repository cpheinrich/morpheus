## Context

Issue #280 showed that removing `agent-reviewed` while a correction is pending reports
an unfinished review even when the committed record is complete. The diagnostic must state
the observed label state without claiming to have validated that record.

## Approach

Keep the existing early refusal and explain missing merge-readiness, skipped record validation,
and when to restore the label. Pin the diagnostic for both complete and incomplete records.

## Acceptance

- Missing label still fails conventions for both complete and incomplete review records.
- The error states the label is absent and record validation was not run, without inferring review outcome.
- Applying the label still invokes all existing record and coverage checks.
