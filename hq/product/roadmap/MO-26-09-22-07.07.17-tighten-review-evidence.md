## Context

A survey of the first two weeks of independent review on Lakina and Evo (163 findings across 75
records) found the gate catching real production bugs that CI passed, and four ways its evidence
could be hollowed out: sub-minute reviews at normal risk (the PR adopting the policy was reviewed
in 42 seconds); reviewer ids the author composed, reused across tasks or permuted from the same
eight characters; a five-minute small-risk ceiling that produced accounting rather than reviews;
and deferred findings with nowhere to be picked up from (a no-year cron deferred and still firing a
week later). Alex's #241 also asked for conditional clearance so a two-line fix does not need a
human. Chris asked on 2026-09-22 for all five.

## Approach

In `check pr`: refuse an initial review under one minute at normal or high risk; require
`reviewerSession` to be a runner-issued id (UUID or 16-plus hex, optional provider prefix) that
appears in no other worklog on the branch; raise the small ceiling to 10 minutes (follow-up 5);
require a `roadmap` id on any `deferred` or `open` finding and check the item exists; and add
reviewer-set `condition` with author `conditionMet`, under which a substantive finding needs no
turn and a final turn may clear with `covered` moving to the fix, verified path by path. Runbook,
prompt, `AGENTS.md`, scaffold template, architecture and the `review prepare` placeholder change
together.

## Acceptance

- A normal-risk record at 0.99 minutes is refused; 1 minute passes; small has no floor.
- An author-chosen reviewer label is refused; a runner id reused in another worklog is refused.
- Small risk passes at 10 minutes and fails at 10.1; small follow-ups at 5 and 5.1.
- A deferred or open finding without a roadmap id, or naming one that does not exist, is refused.
- A substantive finding fixed within a reviewer condition merges without a follow-up; a fix
  outside the condition's paths, or `conditionMet` without a condition, is refused.
