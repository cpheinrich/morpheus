## Context

The consumer-auth extraction (MO-26-08-19-00.39.34, #137) surfaced a conflict: the scaffold ships
Evo's field-verified Resend mail path while the 2026-08-01 decision named Cloudflare Email
Sending canonical for all transactional mail. The inbox put three options to Chris; his answer
cut differently — split by audience, not vendor.

## Approach

Resend is canonical for anything a customer receives (auth mail, receipts, product email), on the
launch-day evidence: 52-minute domain verification, inbox-not-spam delivery from a
no-sending-history domain, and the scaffold's tested `deliver()` semantics. Cloudflare Email
Sending keeps admin and internal mail, where there are no sender-reputation stakes and the
already-in-the-stack vendor wins by default. Updated together: architecture §6 (two rows plus the
prose), the superseded-in-scope 2026-08-01 decision entry, the consumer-auth runbook, the
scaffold's seam comment, the onboarding task, and the inbox item marked settled.
