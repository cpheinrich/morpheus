## Context

Evo PR558 has immutable public Google Storage screenshots under its configured approved
bucket prefix, but the shared visual-evidence check rejects their required
`authuser=<email>` account selector. The Google-link policy already requires this
selector. Removing it or weakening the repository's approved prefix would evade the
conflict rather than fix the shared validator.

## Approach

Permit exactly one email-valued `authuser` query only on approved
`storage.googleapis.com` evidence URLs. Retain HTTPS, exact origin/path scope,
strict descendant paths, no credentials or fragments, and rejection of signed or
arbitrary queries. Reuse native URL parsing and the existing Zod email validator.

## Acceptance

- The actual Evo558 Google Storage screenshot URL passes under its existing prefix.
- One raw or percent-encoded email account selector is accepted on approved Google Storage media.
- Empty, numeric, malformed or duplicate selectors, extra query keys, signed URLs,
  fragments, credentials, sibling buckets and wrong origins remain rejected.
- Other approved providers remain query-free, and approved prefix configuration remains unchanged.
