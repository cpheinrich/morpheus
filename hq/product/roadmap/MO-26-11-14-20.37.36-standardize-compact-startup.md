## Context

The owner asked for Evo's compact startup across Morpheus-managed repositories,
fresh agent verification, and full consolidation of Lakina's decisions and
lessons. Morpheus itself and new scaffolds still request full startup records.

## Approach

Use the existing manifest and receipt protocol. Put rules every session needs
in the declared core, then use cited project search for the task. Short records
live under descriptive headings in the root files. Longer standalone records
remain supported. Lakina will consolidate its existing bodies into the two
root files and move its mandatory startup rules into the core. Deliver each
existing repository through a separately verified change where needed.

## Mandate

The owner authorized implementation, the managed repository rollout, and fresh
session validation. Ordinary repository claim, review, CI and merge controls
apply. Use the current controller and fresh read-only verification or review
sessions. Budget for additional paid model calls or services is zero.

## Acceptance

- Morpheus and new scaffolds declare a working compact core. Missing session
  identity or invalid core selects full canonical reads.
- Existing managed projects adopt or verify the same workflow, preserving
  project-specific mandatory rules.
- Fresh sessions verify the brief, recorded startup reads, and a cited task
  lookup from the assigned checkout. Lakina's migration preserves all record
  bodies and repairs live references to the moved records.
