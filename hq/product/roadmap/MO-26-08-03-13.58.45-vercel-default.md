## Context

Chris, while scaffolding Heinrich LLC: *"you should always use Vercel! That is the Morpheus
default. If its unclear from Morpheus docs make it clear!!!"*

He is right that it is unclear, and there is evidence rather than just an opinion. §10.2 argues
for Vercel entirely in terms of `/hq` dashboards, auth middleware and preview commenting — so a
one-page marketing site reads as a case the section does not cover. Two of three projects that hit
that fork went the other way:

- **Lakina** filed `hosting-cloudflare-pages` as a deviation, reasoning that a single marketing
  page whose only reviewer wrote it earns nothing from Vercel Comments, and Cloudflare already
  held the registrar and DNS.
- **Heinrich LLC** reached the identical fork six weeks later, and only chose Vercel because Chris
  said so out loud.

A default that two projects out of three talk themselves out of is not a default. And the local
reasoning is not wrong — each sentence of it is true — which is why restating the conclusion more
firmly would not have helped.

## Approach

Three changes to `architecture.md`, addressing the gap rather than the volume.

1. **A callout at the top of §10.2**: every web surface, every size, and an agent reading it
   follows rather than weighs.
2. **A "small-site trap" subsection** quoting the reasoning verbatim, granting that it is true,
   and giving the three things it cannot see from inside one project — uniformity is the product;
   "only one page" is a claim about today, and Lakina's own deviation records the capability it
   had already given up; and the marginal account is not the cost it appears to be. Then the bar:
   a deviation needs a reason the next project would not also have, and "small site" describes
   most projects.
3. **The §9 service table row**, which said "decided on the review loop" — the framing that
   invites the exception. Now says every size, and names Cloudflare Pages as the thing it is not.

Also fixes the last line of §10.2: the reconsider conditions are reasons to change the default for
everything, in this file, not to except one project from it.

**Not done:** Lakina's existing deviation is left alone. Whether to migrate that site is Chris's
call, not something to decide by editing a spec.
