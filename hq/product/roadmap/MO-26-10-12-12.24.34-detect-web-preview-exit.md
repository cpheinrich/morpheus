## Context

Issue #352: web preview start polls for the full readiness timeout after the launchd supervisor or dev command has exited.

## Approach

Check supervisor liveness while awaiting readiness, distinguish pending first launch from completed execution, and include a bounded recent log tail on failure. Detect dev commands that exit while the overlay is binding.

## Acceptance

Exited/missing jobs fail promptly with log diagnostics; pending/live jobs keep their readiness opportunity; cancellation and timeout remain bounded; an immediately exiting real dev command terminates its supervisor.
