## Context

The Evo QA overlay can SIGSEGV inside Apple's accessibility bridge when Safari reads its
AX stream. The overlay and simulator stream currently share a supervisor process, so the
crash removes both and interrupts comment QA. Issue #397 records the incident and worklog.

## Approach

Run the overlay in a supervised child process, retry one unexpected exit with accessibility
disabled, and provide `--no-ax` for an explicit screenshot-pin mode. Keep batches and the
simulator stream available through the fallback; test the failure boundary and no-AX routes.
