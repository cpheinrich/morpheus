## Context

Incidental findings from the independent review of MO-26-10-24-18.48.39 (PR #480), both
present before that change in `plugins/codex-claude/src/config.mjs`:

- INC-ALIAS: the moving-alias refusal compares the whole string, so `best[1m]` or
  `default[1m]` passes for an override, a `modelMap` value and now `modelFallback`.
- INC-PROTO: `config.modelMap[snapshot.model]` is not limited to own keys, so a Codex model
  named `toString`, `constructor` or `__proto__` reads a prototype member and fails as an
  unsupported identifier instead of falling back.

## Approach

Strip a bracketed context suffix before the moving-alias check, and look mappings up with
`Object.hasOwn`. Add boundary tests for both.
