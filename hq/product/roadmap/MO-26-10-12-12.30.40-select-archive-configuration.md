## Context

Issue #325: Release is hard-coded in both archive settings queries and archive, and the action rejects conventional spaced scheme names. Evo's production target needs a distinct configuration.

## Approach

Add configuration defaulting to Release, preserve it in job environment and both Xcode paths, and permit spaces in scheme/configuration while refusing blank values and CR/LF injection. Keep path restrictions and explicit archive-build-settings behavior.

## Acceptance

Default callers archive Release; named configurations are used in all settings/archive invocations; spaced names stay one argument; multiline/blank names cannot write job environment. No consumer deployment or activation.
