## Context

PR #267 stopped persistent runners from accumulating abandoned iOS simulators by creating and
deleting a pristine device for each job. Evo's unchanged UI methods then took 1.92× as much
aggregate case time; a near-controlled three-method sample took 2.31×. Combined with a later move
from two workers to serial execution, the test step rose from roughly 32 minutes to 130 minutes.

## Approach

Keep one dedicated, shut-down warmed template on self-hosted runners and clone a unique test
device from it for each job. Serialize template preparation across runner slots, recover
interrupted warm-up, replace superseded runtime/device templates, and preserve the existing
unconditional cleanup of each job clone and its XCTest workers. Keep pristine creation on
ephemeral GitHub-hosted runners.
