## Context

HQ formatting gaps kept recurring across Morpheus projects. Project repositories rarely carry
`docs/runbooks/hq-product-design.md`, yet the shared local review prompt pointed reviewers at that
repo-relative path. The scaffolded `AGENTS.md` and `hq/README.md` did not mention the standard at
all. Material HQ Markdown also usually falls outside `review.visualEvidence` globs, so authors and
reviewers treated rendered evidence as optional.

## Approach

- Give the canonical standard a short author checklist: a recent local page of the same type as
  the model, shared page primitives, next action and owner first, procedure stop/acceptance
  checks, preserved URLs and anchors, and final renders at 1440, 1024 and 390 CSS pixels.
- State that visual materiality is judged on the rendered result regardless of extension. Keep
  that, and the private/synthetic capture rule, separate from automatic `check pr` path matching.
- Point the scaffolded `AGENTS.md`, `hq/README.md`, the shared local review prompt and the VP
  Design HQ reference at the absolute standard URL and `morpheus product guide vp-design/hq-experience`.
- No new CI gate and no automatic rewrite of existing project files; projects pick the guidance up
  through new scaffolds, the installed CLI guide and the review packet.

## Acceptance

- Regression assertions in `tests/agent-instructions.test.ts` and `tests/product-skills.test.ts`
  pin the absolute link, checklist items, widths and the separation from path matching.
