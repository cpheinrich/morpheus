## Context

Evo delivery has stalled despite successive incident repairs. The requested contract
is staging every six hours when iOS inputs change and daily production from source
that has been on main for at least 24 hours, with recovery owning internal TestFlight
availability. Christopher authorized a requirements-first redesign and implementation.

## Approach

Publish a WIP design, review it in a fresh independent session, incorporate the
worklog/run/chat RCA and primary-source research, and test the highest-risk assumptions.
Merge the validated design before implementation. The implementation campaign must
produce eight consecutive real internal deliveries across staging and production;
design approval alone is not operational acceptance.

## Acceptance

- Requirements, reusable/consumer split, failure ownership and evidence are explicit.
- Sanitized RCA and researched alternatives inform the design.
- Independent fresh review findings are resolved and empirical prototypes are recorded.
- Migration, rollback and eight-delivery fault-testing plan are reviewable.
