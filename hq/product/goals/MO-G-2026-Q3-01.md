## Why this goal

Morpheus is only proven when a real project depends on it. Two is the meaningful
number, not one: the first retrofit exposes what the templates are missing, and the
second proves the templates absorbed the lesson. Anything that needed hand-editing
twice is a template bug.

Evo first, then Darwin. Success means both repos build, deploy, and pass CI using
the shared workflows and the published kit.
