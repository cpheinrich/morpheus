# Provenance and first-draft limits

This first draft was requested by Chris on 2026-10-29 after repeated design and information-
hierarchy problems across the Lakina, Darwin and Evo HQ portals. Its executive-first communication,
shared-system canary and evidence-in-appendices rules come from the cross-project HQ audit and the
existing concise-research-record decision. Its mandate, authority, budget and evidence boundaries
follow the established agentic-CTO pattern, adapted to product decisions rather than inherited
technical ownership.

The role deliberately does not define a universal prioritization formula, organization chart,
research method or release process. Projects keep their own goals, authority, data policies and
delivery controls. The skill should be revised after it has led real product outcomes in at least
two projects; until then, treat its portfolio cadence and artifact shapes as a strong starting
contract, not evidence that one operating model fits every product.
